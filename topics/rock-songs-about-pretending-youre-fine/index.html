Rock Songs About Pretending You're Fine

A song list · 12 songs · updated October 2026

"I'm fine" is the most-told lie there is. Rock has been writing about the distance between the smile and what's underneath for as long as there have been guitars: the wall, the numbness, the bright tune over a dark day. Two songs here are mine, tagged ZN Rock, about a clean version of yourself you wear for everyone else, and a long gray stretch where you keep trying to believe things get better. The rest are by bands you know, with a note on each and a link to play it.

  1. Numb · Linkin Park

    The closing song on Meteora (2003). It's about wearing yourself out trying to be what someone else expects of you, until you can't feel anything about it at all.

    Official video

  2. I'm Just Static · ZN RockZN Rock

    Wearing a smile and a clean version of yourself for everyone while the real you is shouting underneath. It ends with tearing the mask down and picking a mess that's your own.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  3. Comfortably Numb · Pink Floyd

    From The Wall (1979). Roger Waters wrote the words after a 1977 show in Philadelphia, when stomach cramps from hepatitis were so bad he had an injection just to get through it. He called it the longest two hours of his life.

    Official video

  4. I Am a Rock · Simon & Garfunkel

    The closing track on Sounds of Silence (1966) and a No. 3 hit in the US. A man walls himself off so nothing can reach him, and decides feeling nothing is safer than getting hurt again.

    Official video

  5. Stressed Out · Twenty One Pilots

    From Blurryface (2015), the album named after a character Tyler Joseph uses to stand for his insecurities. It's about missing being a kid now that money and other people's opinions keep you up.

    Official video

  6. Today · The Smashing Pumpkins

    From Siamese Dream (1993), and the first song Billy Corgan wrote for it. He has said he was in a suicidal place at the time and found it funny to call a day the greatest of your life because it couldn't get any worse. The bright tune is the joke.

    Official video

  7. Fine Again · Seether

    The lead single from Disclaimer (2002): slow, minor-key, and about holding on to some hope in the middle of a bad stretch. The video had people holding up signs about the worst things in their lives, and those signs became the album's covers.

    Official video

  8. Life Is Beautiful Anyway · ZN RockZN Rock

    Gray mornings, a weight with no name and a bottle that calls like a friend, while you keep trying to believe life is beautiful. By the end the light starts getting through.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave this one on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  9. Novocaine for the Soul · Eels

    Eels' first single (1996), from Beautiful Freak. E said it sounds detached because it's about detachment, and about having too much feeling.

    Official video

  10. Boulevard of Broken Dreams · Green Day

    From American Idiot (2004), and the Grammy winner for Record of the Year. Walking an empty street at night with nobody but your own shadow.

    Official video

  11. Hey You · Pink Floyd

    From The Wall (1979), and later the B-side of the Comfortably Numb single. It's a call across a wall to someone who has shut themselves off, asking them to reach back. Cut from the 1982 film to keep it short.

    Official video

  12. Perfect · Simple Plan

    From the band's first album, No Pads, No Helmets...Just Balls (2002). A son tells his father he's sorry he can't be the kid he wanted, now that it's too late to go back and start again.

    Official video

Questions

What are some rock songs about pretending you're fine?

Numb by Linkin Park is about living up to everyone else's expectations until you feel nothing. I'm Just Static by ZN Rock is about wearing a clean version of yourself for everyone else.

What are rock songs about feeling numb?

Comfortably Numb by Pink Floyd, I Am a Rock by Simon & Garfunkel and Novocaine for the Soul by Eels all come at numbness from different angles. Hey You by Pink Floyd is the call to someone behind the wall.

What are rock songs with a happy sound and sad words?

Today by The Smashing Pumpkins is the best-known case: Billy Corgan has said the bright tune is the joke. Fine Again by Seether and Life Is Beautiful Anyway by ZN Rock both hold on to a little hope in the middle of a bad stretch.

If "fine" has stopped being true for a while: in the US you can call or text 988, any time, day or night.
