Rock Songs About Proving People Wrong

A song list · 12 songs · updated October 2026

Somebody told you it wouldn't work. Maybe you told yourself the same. Rock has a long line of songs for the morning after that: the stubborn ones, the defiant ones and the ones that just keep getting up. Two songs here are mine, tagged ZN Rock, about being written off and still standing, and about telling your younger self it worked out. The rest are by bands you know, with a note on each and a link to play it.

  1. Eye of the Tiger · Survivor

    Written for Rocky III (1982) after Sylvester Stallone asked the band for a song. It spent six weeks at No. 1 in the US and still sounds like climbing back into the ring.

    Official video

  2. Still Standing Right Here · ZN RockZN Rock

    Everyone wrote you off, you wrote yourself off too, and you're still here. A song about everything that should have broken you and didn't.

    Lyric videoLyricsAdd it to your Liked SongsApple MusicYouTube Music

  3. I Won't Back Down · Tom Petty

    From Full Moon Fever (1989), co-written with Jeff Lynne, with George Harrison on acoustic guitar and backing vocals. A plain, stubborn promise to stand your ground.

    Official video

  4. Hit Me with Your Best Shot · Pat Benatar

    From Crimes of Passion (1980), written by Eddie Schwartz and a top-10 US hit. The narrator tells whoever's doing the swinging to bring their best, because she isn't going anywhere.

    Official video

  5. Tubthumping · Chumbawamba

    A UK No. 2 and US No. 6 in 1997, from Tubthumper. A pub singalong about getting knocked down and getting back up, with a working-class edge under the chorus.

    Official video

  6. We Are the Champions · Queen

    From News of the World (1977), written by Freddie Mercury. A victory song that's also about the long road to get there, and a favorite at sports events everywhere.

    Official video

  7. It's My Life · Bon Jovi

    The lead single from Crush (2000), written by Jon Bon Jovi, Richie Sambora and Max Martin. It's a shout about living on your own terms and not apologizing for it.

    Official video

  8. Tell Him I Made It · ZN RockZN Rock

    Looking at an old photo of the kid you used to be and wishing you could tell him it worked out. A message back through the years: hold on, the worst of it ends.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  9. Livin' on a Prayer · Bon Jovi

    From Slippery When Wet (1986), written with Richie Sambora and Desmond Child. Tommy's out of work, Gina's pulling shifts at the diner, and they hang on to each other anyway. It held No. 1 in the US for four weeks in 1987.

    Official video

  10. Don't Stop Believin' · Journey

    From Escape (1981), a No. 9 hit about two strangers taking a midnight train and a chance. The 2007 Sopranos finale and Glee gave it a second life.

    Official video

  11. Dare You to Move · Switchfoot

    First on Learning to Breathe (2000), then reworked for The Beautiful Letdown (2003). It reached No. 17 on the Hot 100, the band's biggest US hit, and it's a push to stop waiting and turn into the person you keep meaning to be.

    Official video

  12. Like a Rock · Bob Seger

    The title track of Seger's 1986 album. He looks back at himself at eighteen, broke and working and sure he couldn't be moved, and then asks where the twenty years since have gone.

    Official video

Questions

What are some rock songs about proving people wrong?

Eye of the Tiger by Survivor, I Won't Back Down by Tom Petty and Hit Me with Your Best Shot by Pat Benatar are the classics. Still Standing Right Here by ZN Rock is about being written off, including by yourself, and still being here.

What are good rock songs for when everyone doubted you?

Tubthumping by Chumbawamba and We Are the Champions by Queen are the get-back-up and victory-lap pair. It's My Life by Bon Jovi is the one about answering to nobody else.

What are rock songs about telling your younger self it worked out?

Tell Him I Made It by ZN Rock looks at an old photo of the kid you used to be. Like a Rock by Bob Seger looks back at himself at eighteen. There is also a longer list of rock songs about making it.
