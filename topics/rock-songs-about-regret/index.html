Rock Songs About Regret and Mistakes

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

Half of rock is somebody wishing they'd done it differently: the call they didn't make, the thing they said, the person they let walk out. This list sticks to songs that own up to it. Three are mine, marked ZN Rock, and the last of those takes the other side. Under each one you'll find the story and a link.

  1. How You Remind Me · Nickelback

    The US No. 1 from Silver Side Up (2001) that broke the band worldwide, and the most-played song on US radio in the 2000s. Chad Kroeger wrote it after a fight with his then-girlfriend, and it takes as much of the blame as it hands out.

    Official video

  2. What I've Done · Linkin Park

    The lead single from Minutes to Midnight (2007), also heard in the first Transformers film that summer. It's about facing what you've done and wanting to wipe the slate clean.

    Official video

  3. Nine Years In · ZN RockZN Rock

    One remark at a birthday, one walk-out, then nine years of not picking up the phone. The friend got on with his life, and the only one who ever paid for the grudge was the man holding it.

    Lyric videoLyricsSpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. Against the Wind · Bob Seger

    The title track of Seger's 1980 album, his only No. 1 record, and a No. 5 single. He looks back on a life of running against everything and admits some of what he's learned he'd rather not know.

    Official video

  5. The Scientist · Coldplay

    From A Rush of Blood to the Head (2002). A man who got it wrong wants to go back to the start, and the video tells its story backwards. Chris Martin learned to sing the whole song in reverse to film it.

    Official video

  6. Tell Me Why You Stay · ZN RockZN Rock

    Weeks of silence, a closed door, and someone still in the kitchen putting you first. The regret is in the bridge: a hundred quiet nights you could have said it, and finally saying it.

    Lyric videoLyricsSpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  7. Every Rose Has Its Thorn · Poison

    Bret Michaels has told it many times: on tour, he called his girlfriend from a laundromat, heard a man's voice in the background and wrote this. It spent three weeks at No. 1 in the US in 1988.

    Official video

  8. Don't Know What You Got (Till It's Gone) · Cinderella

    The power ballad from Long Cold Winter (1988) and the band's biggest single, No. 12 in the US. Tom Keifer sings about a love he only valued once it had gone.

    Official video

  9. My Best Bad Idea · ZN RockZN Rock

    The other side of regret. Twenty-two, broke, on a borrowed floor, and everyone swore you'd regret it. You're still waiting for that part, so the song raises a glass to every bad call that turned out good.

    Lyric videoLyricsSpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  10. Wasted Time · Eagles

    From Hotel California (1976), written by Don Henley and Glenn Frey and sung by Henley. Two people after a breakup, both wondering whether the years together were wasted, and hoping one day they'll see they weren't.

    Official video

  11. Perfect · Simple Plan

    From the band's first album, No Pads, No Helmets...Just Balls (2002). A son tells his father he's sorry he can't be the kid he wanted, now that it's too late to go back and start again.

    Official video

  12. Hard to Say I'm Sorry · Chicago

    Written by Peter Cetera and David Foster for Chicago 16 (1982), and a US No. 1. An apology that won't come out easily, and a plea not to leave before it does.

    Official video

  13. Someday · Nickelback

    The lead single from The Long Road (2003), a No. 7 hit in the US. A relationship that has gone sour, and a promise that somehow it'll get put right.

    Official video

  14. Don't Look Back in Anger · Oasis

    From (What's the Story) Morning Glory? (1995), and the first Oasis single sung by Noel Gallagher instead of Liam. A UK No. 1 in 1996 about letting the past go without the bitterness.

    Official video

  15. Good Riddance (Time of Your Life) · Green Day

    From Nimrod (1997). Billie Joe Armstrong wrote it about a girlfriend who was moving to Ecuador. It ended up as the song for graduations and goodbyes, where it means something close to no regrets.

    Official video

Questions

What are some good rock songs about regret?

How You Remind Me by Nickelback, What I've Done by Linkin Park, Against the Wind by Bob Seger and The Scientist by Coldplay are some of the best known. Nine Years In by ZN Rock is newer, about nine years of silence over a grudge.

What's a rock song about regretting how you treated someone?

Every Rose Has Its Thorn by Poison, Hard to Say I'm Sorry by Chicago and Don't Know What You Got by Cinderella. Tell Me Why You Stay by ZN Rock is the thank-you someone should have heard years earlier.

Are there rock songs about having no regrets?

Good Riddance (Time of Your Life) by Green Day and Don't Look Back in Anger by Oasis are the classics. My Best Bad Idea by ZN Rock toasts every wrong turn that worked out.
