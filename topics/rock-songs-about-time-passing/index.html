Rock Songs About Time Passing and Getting Older

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

At some point the years stop feeling like a number and start feeling like a place you left. Rock has been writing about that since its first generation got old: the clock on the wall, the old photo, the town that looks smaller now. Four of these are mine, tagged ZN Rock, about what the years did and didn't take. Every song comes with a note on it and a link to play it.

  1. Time · Pink Floyd

    From The Dark Side of the Moon (1973), with lyrics by Roger Waters. It opens on a wall of ringing clocks and then turns to the shock of realizing the years have gone.

Questions

What are some good rock songs about time passing?

Time by Pink Floyd, Glory Days by Bruce Springsteen, Dream On by Aerosmith and Landslide by Fleetwood Mac are the best known. I Keep Saying I'll Be Happy When by ZN Rock is about the years going by while you wait to be happy.

What are some rock songs about getting older?

Landslide by Fleetwood Mac and Dream On by Aerosmith are both about aging, and Photograph by Nickelback is about looking back at the town you grew up in. Tell Him I Made It by ZN Rock looks back at your younger self.

What are rock songs about missing the good old days?

Summer of '69 by Bryan Adams, The Boys of Summer by Don Henley and Night Moves by Bob Seger all look back at one summer that seemed like it would last.
