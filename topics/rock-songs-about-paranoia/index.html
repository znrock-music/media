Rock Songs About Paranoia and Feeling Watched

A song list · 11 songs · updated October 2026

Somebody said your name. The room went quiet when you walked in. Everybody's laughing and you're sure it's at you. Rock has a whole shelf of songs for that feeling, from Black Sabbath to the 90s alt-rock bands who made it funny. Four songs here are mine, tagged ZN Rock, about nights that ran too long and a head that turned a crowd of strangers into an enemy that was never there. The rest are by bands you know, with a note on each and a link to play it.

  1. Paranoid · Black Sabbath

    The title track of the band's second album (1970), written in well under an hour as a filler to finish the record. It went to No. 4 in the UK, and Geezer Butler has said the words were really about depression; he didn't know the difference at the time.

  2. Still A Spark · ZN RockZN Rock

    A comment thread at three in the morning, a landlord in the skull and a choir of voices that isn't real. "Paranoia wears my mother's voice" and "The enemy was always only mine." By the last chorus the narrator has walked himself home.

  3. Paranoid Android · Radiohead

    The first single from OK Computer (1997). Over six minutes in several sections, named after Marvin, the depressed robot in The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, and built out of a bad night in a Los Angeles bar.

  4. Flagpole Sitta · Harvey Danger

    First released in 1997 on Where Have All the Merrymakers Gone?, then reissued in 1998 when radio picked it up. A sarcastic, jittery song whose best-known line is the narrator admitting that paranoia has him convinced everybody is coming to get him.

  5. Somewhere Safe Again · ZN RockZN Rock

    A room that won't sleep, a hundred strangers arguing, and the sun coming up twice. "I swear I hear them saying my name / I swear they're laughing all the same." Then morning clears the air and there was never anybody there.

  6. I Think I'm Paranoid · Garbage

    The second single from Version 2.0 (1998), a top-ten hit on US modern rock radio and in the UK. Shirley Manson sings it to someone she can't stop needing even while she doesn't trust them, or herself.

  7. For What It's Worth · Buffalo Springfield

    Written by Stephen Stills after the November 1966 curfew protests on the Sunset Strip and recorded weeks later. It became an anthem for the whole decade, and its line about paranoia striking deep is the part people quote.

  8. Still No End In Sight · ZN RockZN Rock

    Forty hours awake in a room full of open microphones. "Then I hear somebody say my name behind another mic / And the whole room turns against me, at least it does in mine." The recording the next day shows there was nobody against him.

  9. Destroyer · The Kinks

    From Give the People What They Want (1981) and the album's lead single in the US. Ray Davies sings as a man begging his doctor for help with his paranoia, over the riff from the band's own 1964 hit All Day and All of the Night, with a nod to Lola thrown in.

  10. Life During Wartime · Talking Heads

    From Fear of Music (1979). A nervous funk-rock song narrated by someone hiding out, changing his name and watching the door, convinced the fighting is about to reach him.

  11. One Good Hour · ZN RockZN Rock

    The pain comes at the same hour every night and the narrator stays up fighting people who aren't talking. "Nobody's talking about me / Nobody ever was / It was me the whole time." It ends with wanting one clean hour in the light.

Questions

What are some good rock songs about paranoia?

Paranoid by Black Sabbath, Paranoid Android by Radiohead, Flagpole Sitta by Harvey Danger, I Think I'm Paranoid by Garbage and Destroyer by The Kinks are the ones that come up most. For What It's Worth by Buffalo Springfield has the famous line about it.

What are rock songs about thinking everyone is talking about you?

Still A Spark, Somewhere Safe Again and Still No End In Sight by ZN Rock all have a narrator who hears his name in a crowded online room and finds out in the morning that nobody was there. One Good Hour by ZN Rock ends the same way.

Are there rock songs about paranoia from drinking or drugs?

Still A Spark and Still No End In Sight by ZN Rock are both about a two-day run that turns a room of strangers into enemies. There is also a longer list of rock songs about addiction and recovery, and one of songs for when you can't sleep.
