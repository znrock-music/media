Rock Songs About Loneliness

A song list · 16 songs · updated October 2026

Loneliness in rock comes in a few shapes: shutting everybody out, being the odd one at the party, a night with nobody to call. These songs cover all of them, and most are classics. Four are mine, tagged ZN Rock, and they look at the other side of it, the people who keep you company at 2 a.m. Each song has a note on what it's about and a link to play it.

  1. I Am a Rock · Simon & Garfunkel

    A 1966 single that reached No. 3 in the US. A man shuts himself in his room with his books and his poetry and decides that if he doesn't love anyone, nobody can hurt him.

    Official video

  2. Eleanor Rigby · The Beatles

    From Revolver (1966), released as a double A-side with Yellow Submarine. The Beatles play no instruments on it, just a string octet under Paul McCartney's voice. It's about a woman who sweeps up rice after weddings and a priest who writes sermons nobody hears.

    Official video

  3. The Thing I'd Never Say · ZN RockZN Rock

    For the voices that keep you company at 2 a.m. without ever asking if you're okay. It says what you'd never say on a mic: you'd be lost without them.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. Creep · Radiohead

    Radiohead's first single, released in September 1992, and the song that put them on the map. The words describe an obsessive attraction to someone way out of reach, and a narrator who feels he doesn't belong in the same room.

    Official video

  5. Hey You · Pink Floyd

    From The Wall (1979), and later the B-side of the Comfortably Numb single. It's a call across a wall to someone who has shut themselves off, asking them to reach back. Cut from the 1982 film to keep it short.

    Official video

  6. Lonely Day · System of a Down

    The second single from Hypnotize (2005), released in 2006. Daron Malakian wrote it and sings lead, and it earned a Grammy nomination for Best Hard Rock Performance.

    Official video

  7. And We're Never Going Down · ZN RockZN Rock

    A loud roll call of a late-night online room, where the regulars are still around at 2 a.m., nobody agrees and nobody fixes anything. What the room does well is not leave anyone on their own.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  8. Alone · Heart

    Written by Billy Steinberg and Tom Kelly, who first recorded it as i-Ten in 1983. Heart's version, on Bad Animals (1987), reached No. 1 in the US and Canada. It's the sound of someone awake at night admitting they don't want to be on their own anymore.

    Official video

  9. Only the Lonely · Roy Orbison

    Written by Orbison and Joe Melson in 1960. It was his first big hit, No. 2 in the US and No. 1 in the UK, and the one that set his trademark sound.

    Official video

  10. How Soon Is Now? · The Smiths

    Written by Morrissey and Johnny Marr. It began as the B-side of William, It Was Really Nothing in 1984, then reached No. 24 in the UK as a single in 1985 and No. 16 on its re-release. It's about going home alone from the club, wanting to be loved.

    Official video

  11. I'm Just Static · ZN RockZN Rock

    The kind of lonely that fits inside a crowd: a smile for the room and a real voice shouting underneath it, all day long. It ends with tearing the mask off.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  12. Isolation · Joy Division

    From Closer (1980), the band's second and last album, released after singer Ian Curtis died that May. It runs on an electronic drum beat from Stephen Morris and a thin keyboard line from Bernard Sumner.

    Official video

  13. Nutshell · Alice in Chains

    From Jar of Flies (1994), the first EP ever to debut at No. 1 on the Billboard 200. Layne Staley sings about fighting a battle on your own with nobody to turn to, and the band opened their 1996 MTV Unplugged show with it.

    Official video

  14. The Sound of Silence · Simon & Garfunkel

    Paul Simon wrote it in 1964 for a quiet acoustic duo record. Producer Tom Wilson later added drums and electric guitar behind the band's back, and it went to No. 1 in the US on January 1, 1966.

    Official video

  15. Not the One Who Goes · ZN RockZN Rock

    The same online room every night, the same argument, the same loud voices. It's about knowing you won't change a mind and staying anyway, for the quiet ones who read along and never say a word.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  16. Message in a Bottle · The Police

    Sting wrote it for Reggatta de Blanc (1979), the band's first UK No. 1. A castaway sends out an SOS, hears nothing for a year, and then finds out everyone else is stranded too.

    Official video

Questions

What are some rock songs about loneliness?

I Am a Rock by Simon & Garfunkel, Eleanor Rigby by The Beatles, Creep by Radiohead, Alone by Heart and Nutshell by Alice in Chains are some of the best known. The Thing I'd Never Say by ZN Rock is about the voices that keep you company late at night.

What's a rock song about feeling lonely in a crowd?

How Soon Is Now? by The Smiths is about leaving the club alone, and Creep by Radiohead is about not fitting in. I'm Just Static by ZN Rock is about the smile you wear in a room while the real you stays quiet.

Are there rock songs about loneliness that aren't depressing?

Message in a Bottle by The Police ends on the idea that everyone else is stranded too. And We're Never Going Down by ZN Rock is a shout-along for a late-night online room where nobody's on their own.
