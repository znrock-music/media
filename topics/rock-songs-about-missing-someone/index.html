Rock Songs About Missing Someone

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

Missing someone covers a lot of ground: a partner in another time zone, a friend who went quiet, someone who died. These are rock songs about all of it, mostly big ones you'll know. Three are mine, under the ZN Rock tag. Each song comes with the story behind it and a link to play it.

  1. Here Without You · 3 Doors Down

    The ballad that made Away from the Sun (2002) a hit. Brad Arnold has named his then-wife as the inspiration, written from the road, a long way from home.

    Official video

  2. I Miss You · blink-182

    From the band's untitled 2003 album, with Mark Hoppus taking the first verse and Tom DeLonge the second over piano, cello and upright bass. There's a nod to Jack and Sally from The Nightmare Before Christmas in it.

    Official video

  3. The Thing I'd Never Say · ZN RockZN Rock

    Friends you've only ever heard through a speaker late at night. The song admits it's missed that room as much as anyone it could reach out and touch, and that it would be lost without them.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. Far Away · Nickelback

    From All the Right Reasons (2005), a No. 8 hit in the US. Missing someone for too long and finally telling them you want them to stay.

    Official video

  5. Faithfully · Journey

    From Frontiers (1983). Jonathan Cain started the words on a napkin on the tour bus and finished it on a backstage piano the next day, about life on the road and the marriage waiting at home.

    Official video

  6. I Keep Saying I'll Be Happy When · ZN RockZN Rock

    A man you came up with goes early in the year, and from the back of the room you hear what people miss about him: his Sundays, the way he stayed. You go home and call somebody.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  7. Nothing Else Matters · Metallica

    From the Black Album (1991). James Hetfield wrote it on tour while on the phone with his girlfriend, and at first it wasn't meant to be a Metallica song at all.

    Official video

  8. I Don't Want to Miss a Thing · Aerosmith

    Written by Diane Warren for Armageddon (1998). It entered the US chart at No. 1, the band's only No. 1 single.

    Official video

  9. Tell Him I Made It · ZN RockZN Rock

    Missing the kid you used to be: a beat-up jacket in an old photograph, and everything you'd tell him if you could call down the line.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  10. Tears in Heaven · Eric Clapton

    Clapton wrote it with Will Jennings after his four-year-old son Conor died in 1991, and it won the Grammy for Song of the Year. This is the acoustic version from his Unplugged show.

    Official video

  11. My Immortal · Evanescence

    From Fallen (2003). Amy Lee and Ben Moody wrote it as teenagers: someone who's gone but won't leave, still there in every memory.

    Official video

  12. Wherever You Will Go · The Calling

    From Camino Palmero (2001), and 23 weeks at No. 1 on Billboard's Adult Top 40. Co-writer Aaron Kamin has said it came from seeing a widower at a funeral.

    Official video

  13. If You're Gone · Matchbox Twenty

    From Mad Season (2000). Rob Thomas wrote it soon after meeting his future wife, while they were apart and he worried the band's life would make it too hard.

    Official video

  14. Beth · Kiss

    From Destroyer (1976), sung by drummer Peter Criss, and Kiss's biggest US hit at No. 7. It started as Beck, about the wife of a bandmate from Criss's earlier group calling him home from rehearsal.

    Official video

  15. Walking After You · Foo Fighters

    From The Colour and the Shape (1997), re-recorded the next year for The X-Files film soundtrack. A quiet one about someone leaving and not being ready to let them go.

    Official video

Questions

What are some rock songs about missing someone you love?

Here Without You by 3 Doors Down, I Miss You by blink-182, Far Away by Nickelback and Faithfully by Journey are the best known. The Thing I'd Never Say by ZN Rock is about missing friends you've never met.

What rock songs are about missing someone who died?

Tears in Heaven by Eric Clapton, My Immortal by Evanescence and Wherever You Will Go by The Calling. I Keep Saying I'll Be Happy When by ZN Rock starts at a friend's funeral.

Is there a song about missing your old self?

Tell Him I Made It by ZN Rock is written to the kid in an old photo, the one you'd call if you could.

If missing someone has turned into something heavier: in the US you can call or text 988, at any hour.
