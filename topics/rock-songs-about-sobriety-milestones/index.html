Rock Songs About Sobriety Milestones

A song list · 13 songs · updated October 2026

Sobriety is counted in small numbers: the first night, the first week, one day at a time. These are rock songs about those markers, from artists who wrote about their own recovery and from songs that just fit the count. Three of them are mine, tagged ZN Rock. They are songs about trying again, and they make no claims beyond what the words say. Each song has a note and a link to play it.

  1. One Day at a Time · Joe Walsh

    From Analog Man (2012), his first solo album since getting sober. Walsh took the title from the Alcoholics Anonymous mantra, and the song is about his own recovery.

    Official video

  2. Truce for Tonight · ZN RockZN Rock

    Years of swearing today is the day and losing it by midnight. The song gives up the fight to win and makes a smaller deal: one day standing, just today.

    Lyric videoLyricsAdd it to your Liked SongsApple MusicYouTube Music

  3. Just for Today · George Harrison

    From Cloud Nine (1987). Harrison based it on an Alcoholics Anonymous leaflet that friends who had stopped drinking showed him, and the first verse follows its opening lines.

    Official video

  4. Amazing · Aerosmith

    From Get a Grip (1993), co-written by Steven Tyler, who went through rehab in 1986. It looks back at the bad years from the far side and treats getting clean as a second shot at life.

    Official video

  5. Under the Bridge · Red Hot Chili Peppers

    From Blood Sugar Sex Magik (1991). Anthony Kiedis wrote it about the loneliness of getting clean, and about the place in Los Angeles where he had used and nearly lost everything.

    Official video

  6. Still No End In Sight · ZN RockZN Rock

    A weekend that turns into two days awake, and a mind that turns a room against you that was never against you. It ends with one day down and saying out loud that you can't do it alone.

    Lyric videoLyricsIf it sticks, save it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  7. How You Gonna See Me Now · Alice Cooper

    The lead single from From the Inside (1978), a concept album about Cooper's stay in a New York sanitarium to treat his drinking. He's coming home, afraid of how the woman waiting for him will see him after he let her down.

    Official video

  8. Scar Tissue · Red Hot Chili Peppers

    The lead single from Californication (1999), and the Grammy winner for Best Rock Song. Anthony Kiedis wrote it about what addiction leaves behind, and he later gave his memoir the same name.

    Official video

  9. Life Is Beautiful · Sixx:A.M.

    The band's first single (2007), from the soundtrack to Nikki Sixx's book The Heroin Diaries, which grew out of his journals from the worst year of his addiction.

    Official video

  10. It's Been Awhile · Staind

    Staind's biggest hit, from Break the Cycle (2001). Aaron Lewis looks back at addiction, a relationship he wrecked, and how much of the damage he did to himself.

    Official video

  11. One Good Hour · ZN RockZN Rock

    A pain that shows up at the same time every night, and the wish for one clean hour where nothing hurts. It moves from wanting to trade everything for that hour to spending one hour right, then another.

    Lyric videoLyricsIf it sticks, save it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  12. Animal I Have Become · Three Days Grace

    The lead single from One-X (2006), the album Adam Gontier wrote after going through rehab. It's about not recognising who you turn into when you're using.

    Official video

  13. Monsters · Shinedown

    A No. 1 on Billboard's Mainstream Rock chart from Attention Attention (2018), an album that follows one man fighting his demons inside a single room.

    Official video

Questions

What are some rock songs about sobriety?

One Day at a Time by Joe Walsh, Just for Today by George Harrison and Amazing by Aerosmith were all written by musicians who had stopped drinking. Truce for Tonight by ZN Rock is about asking for just one day.

What are rock songs about one day at a time?

One Day at a Time by Joe Walsh takes its title from the Alcoholics Anonymous mantra, and Just for Today by George Harrison is built on an AA leaflet. Truce for Tonight by ZN Rock makes the same small deal.

What are rock songs about the first night clean?

Under the Bridge and Scar Tissue by the Red Hot Chili Peppers both come from Anthony Kiedis's recovery, and Still No End In Sight by ZN Rock ends on one day down. There is also a longer list of rock songs about getting sober.

If you're trying to get sober and want help: in the US, SAMHSA's National Helpline (1-800-662-4357) is free, confidential and open 24 hours a day, every day of the year.
