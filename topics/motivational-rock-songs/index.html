Motivational Rock Songs

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

Some songs get you out of the chair. This is a list of the ones people actually put on before a shift, a workout or a hard conversation, mostly big riffs and bigger choruses. Four are mine, tagged ZN Rock, and they're the slower kind of motivation: the kind you need on a day when nothing's going to plan. Every song has a note on it and a link to play it.

  1. Eye of the Tiger · Survivor

    Written by Jim Peterik and Frankie Sullivan for Rocky III (1982) after Sylvester Stallone asked for a theme. It spent six weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 and won the Grammy for Best Rock Performance by a Duo or Group.

  2. Thunderstruck · AC/DC

    The opening track on The Razors Edge (1990), written by Angus and Malcolm Young. It starts with a single guitar line that keeps climbing, and it has been a stadium and gym staple ever since.

    Official video

Questions

What are some good motivational rock songs?

Eye of the Tiger by Survivor, Thunderstruck and Back in Black by AC/DC, Don't Stop Me Now by Queen and It's My Life by Bon Jovi are the usual picks. Still Standing Right Here by ZN Rock is a quieter one, about getting back up after being counted out.

What are good motivational rock songs for work?

It's My Life by Bon Jovi, Best of You by Foo Fighters and Hit Me with Your Best Shot by Pat Benatar work well for a morning when you need to get going. You Don't Get My Day by ZN Rock is for the day when something is trying to take it from you.

What are good rock songs for the gym?

Thunderstruck, Welcome to the Jungle, Seven Nation Army and Believer are all fast, loud and built around a riff that keeps a set going.
