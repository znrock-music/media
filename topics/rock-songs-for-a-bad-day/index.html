Rock Songs for a Bad Day

A song list · 12 songs · updated October 2026

Some days the only plan is to reach the evening. These are rock songs for that kind of day: the ones that say it will pass, the ones that sit with you until it does and the ones that settle for a smaller win. Four songs here are mine, tagged ZN Rock, about a day you refuse to hand over, an hour of relief and a bar so low that getting through counts. The rest are by bands you know, with a note on each and a link to play it.

  1. Hey Jude · The Beatles

    Written by Paul McCartney in 1968 to comfort John Lennon's son Julian during his parents' divorce. Over seven minutes long, and it spent nine weeks at number one in the US.

  2. You Don't Get My Day · ZN RockZN Rock

    A pain that moved in and will not leave, and a narrator who lets it have the room but not the day. The chorus ends on "But you don't get my day."

  3. You Can't Always Get What You Want · The Rolling Stones

    From Let It Bleed (1969), with the London Bach Choir opening the track. A shrug of a song that turns out to be a comfort.

  4. Everybody Hurts · R.E.M.

    From Automatic for the People (1992). The band has said it was aimed at teenagers, and it has been played to a lot of grown-ups on a lot of bad nights since.

  5. Nobody Gets to Keep It · ZN RockZN Rock

    The good stretch never lasts, and neither does the bad one. He ends up counting the bad days as the space between the good ones and gives up chasing forever.

  6. Good Riddance (Time of Your Life) · Green Day

    From Nimrod (1997). Billie Joe Armstrong wrote it for an ex-girlfriend who moved abroad, and it has since turned into the song people play at graduations and goodbyes.

  7. Patience · Guns N' Roses

    From G N' R Lies (1988). A slow acoustic song with whistling, about waiting a rough patch out rather than forcing it.

  8. One Good Hour · ZN RockZN Rock

    The pain shows up on schedule every night, and all he asks for is one clean hour. By the last chorus he is learning to count them one at a time, then another.

  9. Fix You · Coldplay

    From X&Y (2005). Chris Martin wrote it for his wife Gwyneth Paltrow after her father died. It starts quiet and ends big.

  10. Don't Stop Believin' · Journey

    From Escape (1981). Steve Perry, Jonathan Cain and Neal Schon wrote it, and it has outlived its original chart run by decades.

  11. Truce for Tonight · ZN RockZN Rock

    Fourteen years of fighting and a new deal: one day at a time. The plan is to take the dogs out, feel the sun and call it a good one if he gets through this one.

  12. Vienna · Billy Joel

    From The Stranger (1977). Joel has said he wrote it after seeing an old woman sweeping a street in Vienna, and the song is mostly a reminder to slow down.

Questions

What are good rock songs for a bad day?

Hey Jude by The Beatles, You Can't Always Get What You Want by The Rolling Stones, Everybody Hurts by R.E.M., Patience by Guns N' Roses and Fix You by Coldplay are the ones that come up most.

What are rock songs about getting through one hard day?

Truce for Tonight by ZN Rock is about taking one day at a time, and One Good Hour by ZN Rock asks for a single clean hour. Good Riddance by Green Day is the older goodbye-and-keep-going song.

What are rock songs about a day that goes wrong all the way through?

You Don't Get My Day and Nobody Gets to Keep It by ZN Rock both make room for the bad day without letting it win. Patience by Guns N' Roses is the slow one for waiting it out.
