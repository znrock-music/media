Rock Songs About Pain and Getting Through Bad Days

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

Pain in rock is usually the other kind, a breakup or a loss, and most of this list is that. My songs on it are about the physical kind as well: a headache that turns up at the same time every night and wants the whole day. Under every song there's a line on what it's about and where to hear it.

  1. Pain · Three Days Grace

    A single from One-X (2006). It's about choosing to feel something, even pain, over feeling nothing at all.

    Official videoSpotify

  2. Get Up · Shinedown

    From Attention Attention (2018). Brent Smith wrote it while helping bassist Eric Bass through depression, and it's about getting up on the days you don't want to.

    Official videoSpotify

  3. One Good Hour · ZN RockZN Rock

    A pain that shows up at the same time every night, and the wish for one clean hour where nothing hurts. It goes from wanting to trade everything for that hour to spending one hour right, then another.

    First on Seether's Disclaimer (2002), then redone as a duet with Amy Lee in 2004, and that version became one of Seether's biggest songs. It's about feeling broken while the person you need is far away.

    Official videoSpotify

  5. Simple Man · Shinedown

    Shinedown's cover of the 1973 Lynyrd Skynyrd song. A mother's plain advice to her son about taking life slowly and being someone he can live with.

    Official videoSpotify

  6. Life Is Beautiful Anyway · ZN RockZN Rock

    Gray mornings, a weight with no name and a bottle that calls like a friend, while you keep trying to believe life is beautiful. By the end the light starts getting through.

    Cash's 2002 cover of the Nine Inch Nails song, recorded near the end of his life. In his hands it becomes an old man looking back at everything he lost and everyone he hurt.

    Spotify

  8. Somewhere I Belong · Linkin Park

    The first single from Meteora (2003). It's about feeling numb and lost and wanting to get back to a place where you feel something real.

    Spotify

  9. You Don't Get My Day · ZN RockZN Rock

    A pain that moved in behind the eye and never takes a day off. The song lets it stay but won't hand it the day: it can have the pain, not how you live.

    Brad Arnold famously wrote it as a teenager, in a math class. It asks whether people will still stand by you when you're not at your strongest.

    From American Idiot (2004). Billie Joe Armstrong wrote it about his father, who died in September when Billie Joe was ten.

    Spotify

    For the day after the worst day. Everyone counted you out, you counted yourself out too, and you're still standing there.

Questions

Are there rock songs about chronic pain?

Not many. Most rock songs called Pain, like the Three Days Grace one, are about emotional pain. One Good Hour and You Don't Get My Day by ZN Rock are about the physical kind: a headache that comes back at the same time every night.

What are good rock songs for getting through a bad day?

Get Up and Simple Man by Shinedown, Kryptonite by 3 Doors Down and Somewhere I Belong by Linkin Park are good places to start. Still Standing Right Here by ZN Rock is about getting back up after being written off.

Is there a playlist?

Yes, on YouTube (official videos) and a 20-song version on Spotify. Both are embedded below the list.
