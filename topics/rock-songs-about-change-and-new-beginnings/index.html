Rock Songs About Change and New Beginnings

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

Change in rock is rarely tidy. Sometimes it's a whole world turning over, sometimes it's one person finally leaving, and sometimes it's just the first morning that feels different. These songs cover all of them. Four are mine, tagged ZN Rock, about starting over after a rough stretch. Every song comes with a note on what it's about and a link to play it.

  1. Changes · David Bowie

    From Hunky Dory (1971), written by Bowie. The famous stuttered chorus sets the tone: it's about reinvention, and about turning into someone else more than once.

    Official video

  2. Wind of Change · Scorpions

    From Crazy World (1990), written by Klaus Meine after the band played the Moscow Music Peace Festival in 1989. It became the soundtrack of the end of the Cold War and reached No. 4 in the US.

    Official video

  3. I Keep Saying I'll Be Happy When · ZN RockZN Rock

    Always one more year and one more round of work before you let yourself be happy. A funeral for an old friend ends it, and the song stops waiting and puts a date on the list.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. The Times They Are a-Changin' · Bob Dylan

    The title track of the 1964 album, written in 1963. It tells people to move with the times or get out of the way, and it's sung like a warning.

    Official video

  5. (Just Like) Starting Over · John Lennon

    From Double Fantasy (1980), Lennon's first single after five years away from music. It came out two months before his death in December 1980, and then went to No. 1.

    Official video

  6. Closing Time · Semisonic

    From Feeling Strangely Fine (1998), written by Dan Wilson. It works as a last call at a bar and, as Wilson has said, also as a song about a child on the way.

    Official video

  7. Somewhere Safe Again · ZN RockZN Rock

    A room full of arguing strangers at three in the morning, and the powder and the bottle doing the talking. It ends on digging for one spark and walking yourself back somewhere safe.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave this one on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  8. Don't Stop · Fleetwood Mac

    From Rumours (1977), written by Christine McVie. It's a song about leaving yesterday behind and looking at what's next, and it was later used as the campaign song of Bill Clinton's 1992 run for president.

    Official video

  9. Right Here, Right Now · Jesus Jones

    From Doubt (1991), written by Mike Edwards, who has talked about watching the old Soviet bloc change on TV. It reached No. 2 on the Hot 100.

    Official video

  10. Beautiful Day · U2

    From All That You Can't Leave Behind (2000), and the winner of the Grammy for Record of the Year in 2001. It's about someone who's lost nearly everything and still sees what's left.

    Official video

  11. My Long Way Around · ZN RockZN Rock

    Every wrong road and dead-end town, and no apology for any of them. A song about keeping the whole hard way you came instead of trading it for a cleaner route.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  12. Here Comes the Sun · The Beatles

    From Abbey Road (1969), written by George Harrison. He wrote it in Eric Clapton's garden, after dodging a business meeting, on the first warm day after a bleak winter.

    Official video

  13. Learn to Fly · Foo Fighters

    From There Is Nothing Left to Lose (1999). The video turns the band into the crew and passengers of a plane, with Jack Black and Kyle Gass along for the ride.

    Official video

  14. Born to Run · Bruce Springsteen

    The title track of the 1975 album, and the one that put Springsteen on the map. A couple in a small town talk about getting into a car and not coming back.

    Official video

  15. Tell Him I Made It · ZN RockZN Rock

    A look at an old photo of the kid you used to be, and what you'd say if you could talk to him. Hold on, the worst of it ends.

Questions

What are some good rock songs about change?

Changes by David Bowie, Wind of Change by Scorpions, The Times They Are a-Changin' by Bob Dylan and Right Here, Right Now by Jesus Jones are the best known. I Keep Saying I'll Be Happy When by ZN Rock is about putting off a change until it can't wait.

What are some rock songs about starting over?

(Just Like) Starting Over by John Lennon and Don't Stop by Fleetwood Mac are about moving forward, and Here Comes the Sun by The Beatles is the first warm day after a long winter. Somewhere Safe Again by ZN Rock is about starting over after the worst nights.

What's a rock song about a new beginning?

Beautiful Day by U2 is about someone who has lost almost everything and still sees what's left. Closing Time by Semisonic is a bar's last call and, Dan Wilson has said, a song about a child on the way.
