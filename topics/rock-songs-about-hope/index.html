Rock Songs About Hope

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

Hope in a rock song is hardly ever sunny. It tends to turn up late, after the bad news, sounding a bit worn out and still not willing to quit. That's the kind on this list, from The Beatles and U2 to Oasis and Whitesnake. Four are mine, tagged ZN Rock, for the stretch before things get better. Each one has a short note and a link to play it.

  1. Hey Jude · The Beatles

    A 1968 single Paul McCartney wrote to comfort John Lennon's young son Julian while his parents were splitting up. It spent nine weeks at No. 1 in the US, and the sing-along ending runs for over four minutes.

    Official video

  2. Here I Go Again · Whitesnake

    Written by David Coverdale and Bernie Marsden and first released in 1982, then re-recorded for the 1987 album Whitesnake. That second version went to No. 1 on the Hot 100, a song about deciding to keep going alone rather than stand still.

    Official video

  3. Tell Him I Made It · ZN RockZN Rock

    A grown man talks to the kid in an old photograph, the one with empty pockets who kept going anyway. What he'd send back is the hopeful part: it gets better, and the kid gets there.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. I Still Haven't Found What I'm Looking For · U2

    From The Joshua Tree (1987), and U2's second No. 1 in a row in the US. It leans on gospel, with the Edge, Brian Eno and Daniel Lanois singing the choir parts, and it's about still searching without giving up on finding.

    Official video

  5. Don't Give Up · Peter Gabriel feat. Kate Bush

    A duet from So (1986), inspired by Dorothea Lange's Depression-era photographs and by unemployment in 1980s Britain. Gabriel sings as a man who has lost his job, and Kate Bush is the voice telling him to hold on.

    Official video

  6. Shine · Collective Soul

    The band's debut single (1993), written by Ed Roland. It sat at No. 1 on Billboard's rock chart for eight weeks, and Dean Roland has called its chorus basically a prayer.

    Official video

  7. Still Standing Right Here · ZN RockZN Rock

    Counted out by everyone, the guy in the mirror included, and still on his feet when the dust settles. The hope in this one is stubborn, not pretty.

    Lyric videoLyricsAdd it to your Liked SongsApple MusicYouTube Music

  8. The Rising · Bruce Springsteen

    The title track of his 2002 album, written in response to the September 11 attacks and told through a firefighter climbing one of the towers. It won Grammys for Best Rock Song and Best Male Rock Vocal Performance.

    Official video

  9. Stop Crying Your Heart Out · Oasis

    Written by Noel Gallagher and sung by Liam, from Heathen Chemistry (2002), and a No. 2 hit in the UK. A slow song with an orchestra behind it, telling someone to stop worrying and let things come round.

    Official video

  10. Better Days · Goo Goo Dolls

    The first single from Let Love In (2005), first recorded for a Christmas album. In 2006 the band played it before the New Orleans Saints' first game back in the Superdome after Hurricane Katrina.

    Official video

  11. One Good Hour · ZN RockZN Rock

    A pain that turns up on schedule every night, and a man who stops asking for a cure and asks for one hour instead. Getting that hour, then one more, is as hopeful as it gets, and it's enough.

    Lyric videoLyricsIf it sticks, save it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  12. Dare You to Move · Switchfoot

    First on Learning to Breathe (2000), then reworked for The Beautiful Letdown (2003). It reached No. 17 on the Hot 100, the band's biggest US hit, and it's a push to stop waiting and turn into the person you keep meaning to be.

    Official video

  13. Keep the Faith · Bon Jovi

    The title track and lead single of the 1992 album, written by Jon Bon Jovi, Richie Sambora and Desmond Child. It topped Billboard's Album Rock Tracks chart and is still a live staple, with Jon on percussion.

    Official video

  14. Land of Hope and Dreams · Bruce Springsteen

    Played live from 1999 and only recorded in the studio for Wrecking Ball (2012). It flips the old gospel train song so that everybody gets a seat, and the studio version carries a Clarence Clemons saxophone solo, possibly his last on a Springsteen album.

    Official video

  15. Life Is Beautiful Anyway · ZN RockZN Rock

    Blinds down, the same gray ceiling every morning, and a bottle offering company. The title gets said like a dare to yourself, and by the last chorus some light is coming through.

Questions

What are some good rock songs about hope?

Hey Jude by The Beatles, Here I Go Again by Whitesnake, Don't Give Up by Peter Gabriel and Kate Bush and Shine by Collective Soul are among the best known. Tell Him I Made It by ZN Rock is a message of hope sent back to your younger self.

What are rock songs about hope in hard times?

The Rising by Bruce Springsteen was written after September 11, and Don't Give Up and Stop Crying Your Heart Out by Oasis are both about holding on when things are bad. Still Standing Right Here by ZN Rock is about still being there after everyone wrote you off.

What are some classic rock songs about hope?

Hey Jude by The Beatles, I Still Haven't Found What I'm Looking For by U2 and Keep the Faith by Bon Jovi are classics. Land of Hope and Dreams by Bruce Springsteen has it right in the title.
