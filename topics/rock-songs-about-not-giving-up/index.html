Rock Songs About Not Giving Up

A song list · 16 songs · updated October 2026

For when you've been knocked flat and you're still deciding whether to get up. Some of these are the anthems you've heard at every ballgame, and some came out of real loss. Four are mine and carry the ZN Rock tag. Every song has a short note on what's behind it and a link to play it.

  1. Eye of the Tiger · Survivor

    Written for Rocky III (1982) after Sylvester Stallone asked the band for a song. It spent six weeks at No. 1 in the US and still sounds like climbing back into the ring.

    Official video

  2. I Won't Back Down · Tom Petty

    From Full Moon Fever (1989), co-written with Jeff Lynne, with George Harrison on acoustic guitar and backing vocals. A plain, stubborn promise to stand your ground.

    Official video

  3. Still Standing Right Here · ZN RockZN Rock

    Counted out before the bell, and part of the voice saying you'd never crawl back was your own. Everything that should have ended you didn't, and the chorus plants both feet and stays put.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. Livin' on a Prayer · Bon Jovi

    From Slippery When Wet (1986), written with Richie Sambora and Desmond Child. Tommy's out of work, Gina's pulling shifts at the diner, and they hang on to each other anyway. It held No. 1 in the US for four weeks in 1987.

    Official video

  5. Times Like These · Foo Fighters

    From One by One (2002). Dave Grohl wrote it in 2001, when the band came close to splitting up, about learning how to start over.

    Official video

  6. Life Is Beautiful Anyway · ZN RockZN Rock

    Closed blinds, a weight with no name, and a bottle offering to hold you up. It keeps losing the fight and won't stay down, dragging itself toward the dawn until some light gets in.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  7. The Show Must Go On · Queen

    The closing track on Innuendo (1991), mostly written by Brian May. Freddie Mercury was very ill by then and sang it anyway, and the single came out about six weeks before he died.

    Official video

  8. It's Not My Time · 3 Doors Down

    The lead single from the band's self-titled 2008 album, which topped Billboard's Mainstream Rock chart. Someone who's taken a beating says he isn't going anywhere yet.

    Official video

  9. Tell Him I Made It · ZN RockZN Rock

    Written to the kid in an old photo, back when there was nothing in his pockets. Keep going, it says, the worst of it ends, and you're already the person you'll grow into.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  10. Hero · Chad Kroeger feat. Josey Scott

    The Nickelback singer's song for the Spider-Man soundtrack (2002), with Josey Scott of Saliva, and a No. 3 hit in the US. It's about not waiting around for someone to come and save you.

    Official video

  11. Rise Above This · Seether

    From Finding Beauty in Negative Spaces (2007). Shaun Morgan wrote it about his brother Eugene, who died by suicide that year, and about finding a way through the grief.

    Official video

  12. You Don't Get My Day · ZN RockZN Rock

    A pain that's there before breakfast and still there at lights out. The song gives it a room and a chair in the house and keeps everything else: it can stay, but it doesn't get the day.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  13. Don't Stop Believin' · Journey

    From Escape (1981), a No. 9 hit about two strangers taking a midnight train and a chance. The 2007 Sopranos finale and Glee gave it a second life.

    Official video

  14. Carry On Wayward Son · Kansas

    Kerry Livgren brought it in as the last song for Leftoverture (1976), and it became the band's first Top 40 hit, No. 11 in 1977. A worn-out wanderer is told to keep going and that there'll be peace at the end.

    Official video

  15. Hold On · Good Charlotte

    From The Young and the Hopeless (2002). The video has people talking about losing someone to suicide and about surviving it, and the message is just the title: hold on.

    Official video

  16. Tubthumping · Chumbawamba

    A UK No. 2 and US No. 6 in 1997, from Tubthumper. A pub singalong about getting knocked down and getting back up, with a working-class edge under the chorus.

    Official video

Questions

What are the best rock songs about not giving up?

Eye of the Tiger by Survivor, I Won't Back Down by Tom Petty, Livin' on a Prayer by Bon Jovi and Times Like These by Foo Fighters are the usual picks. Still Standing Right Here by ZN Rock is a newer one about getting up after everyone counted you out.

What's a good rock song for when you feel like giving up?

Hold On by Good Charlotte and Rise Above This by Seether both came out of real loss. Life Is Beautiful Anyway by ZN Rock is about the gray mornings when even getting up is a fight.

Is there a Nickelback song about not giving up?

Hero, Chad Kroeger's 2002 song with Josey Scott for Spider-Man, is the closest. It's about not waiting for someone else to save you.
