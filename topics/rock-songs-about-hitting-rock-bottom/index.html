Rock Songs About Hitting Rock Bottom

A song list · 11 songs · updated October 2026

Rock bottom is a strange place to write from, because the songs usually come later. Some of these were written in the low spot and some from the far side of it, and all of them know the floor by name. Three songs here are mine, tagged ZN Rock, about being counted out, about a long night with no end and about dragging yourself toward morning. The rest are by bands you know, with a note on each and a link to play it.

  1. Hurt · Nine Inch Nails

    The closing track of The Downward Spiral (1994), written by Trent Reznor. Johnny Cash recorded his own version for American IV in 2002, and the two versions are now played side by side as the same song heard from two ends of a life.

  2. Still Standing Right Here · ZN RockZN Rock

    Written off before the bell and still on his feet. The chorus says "I've been to the bottom, I know the sound," and the bridge adds that everything that was supposed to end him did not.

  3. Down in a Hole · Alice in Chains

    From Dirt (1992), written by Jerry Cantrell. A slow, heavy song about being low and wanting out of it, and one of the quieter tracks on a loud record.

  4. It's Been Awhile · Staind

    The lead single from Break the Cycle (2001) and a top-five hit in the US. Aaron Lewis sings it as a man who has let people down and can finally say so.

  5. Life Is Beautiful Anyway · ZN RockZN Rock

    A gray ceiling, shut blinds and a bottle that sounds like a friend. The line to keep is "I'll drag myself toward the dawn," and the last chorus says the cracks are where the light gets in.

  6. Fell on Black Days · Soundgarden

    From Superunknown (1994), written by Chris Cornell. About the day a good stretch turns and you cannot say why, which is a different kind of bottom from the usual one.

  7. Fade to Black · Metallica

    From Ride the Lightning (1984). James Hetfield has said it came out of a bad stretch for the band when their gear was stolen. It was the group's first long ballad and fans took it as their own.

  8. Not a Word I Own · ZN RockZN Rock

    A run that only ends when there is nothing left to take. He admits he never learned the word for enough, and the last chorus still lands on being up and awake.

  9. Animal I Have Become · Three Days Grace

    The lead single from One-X (2006). Widely read as a song about losing control to something you cannot put down, and it is still one of the band's best-known songs.

  10. Comfortably Numb · Pink Floyd

    From The Wall (1979), written by Roger Waters and David Gilmour. Waters sings the verses, Gilmour sings the choruses and plays both solos, and it is the track most people remember from the record.

  11. I Won't Back Down · Tom Petty

    From Full Moon Fever (1989), co-produced with Jeff Lynne, with George Harrison on guitar and backing vocals. A plain, stubborn song about holding your ground when the odds are bad.

Questions

What are some rock songs about hitting rock bottom?

Hurt by Nine Inch Nails (and Johnny Cash's version), Down in a Hole by Alice in Chains, It's Been Awhile by Staind, Fade to Black by Metallica and Comfortably Numb by Pink Floyd are the ones that come up most.

What are rock songs about hitting rock bottom and getting back up?

Still Standing Right Here and Life Is Beautiful Anyway by ZN Rock both end on the other side of the floor. I Won't Back Down by Tom Petty is the older, plainer version of the same idea.

Are there rock songs about a binge that does not stop?

Not a Word I Own by ZN Rock is built on a run that ends only when the body does. Animal I Have Become by Three Days Grace is the one most people name for losing control. There is also a longer list of rock songs about addiction and recovery.
