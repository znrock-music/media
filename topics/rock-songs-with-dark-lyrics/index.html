Rock Songs With Dark Lyrics

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

Dark lyrics, not dark costumes. Most of these are 2000s rock songs you already know by heart: a ghost that won't leave, a man turning into an animal, a voice from hell, a pile of bones. Some came out of things that really happened to the people who wrote them. Four are mine, tagged ZN Rock. Fine for October nights, but none of them is a Halloween song.

  1. My Immortal · Evanescence

    The third single from Fallen (2003), written by Amy Lee and Ben Moody when they were teenagers. It's about someone who is gone and a memory that won't let go.

    Official video

  2. So Cold · Breaking Benjamin

    The lead single from We Are Not Alone (2004), and the record holder for most weeks on Billboard's Mainstream Rock chart, 62. Benjamin Burnley wrote it during a New York winter, cut off from everyone, with the film 28 Days Later on his mind.

    Official video

  3. I'm Just Static · ZN RockZN Rock

    A smile worn like a coat, a clean life stretched over the face, and the man underneath clawing at the seams to get out. It ends with tearing the whole act down and choosing a mess that's your own.

  4. Animal I Have Become · Three Days Grace

    The first single from One-X (2006), seven weeks at No. 1 on the Mainstream Rock chart. Adam Gontier wrote it in rehab, about being angry, out of control and no longer knowing who he was, and realizing he had to ask for help.

    Official video

  5. Leave Out All the Rest · Linkin Park

    From Minutes to Midnight (2007), a single in 2008. It pictures the end of a life and asks to be remembered for the good parts. Chester Bennington said it was meant to read like an apology letter.

    Official video

  6. Inside the Fire · Disturbed

    A No. 1 Mainstream Rock single from Indestructible (2008). David Draiman based it on a girlfriend who took her own life when he was a teenager, and the devil urging him to follow her. The video opens with a suicide hotline number.

    Official video

  7. Still A Spark · ZN RockZN Rock

    Three in the morning, a choir in your head you can't tune, paranoia talking in a familiar voice, and buying quiet with the thing that makes it worse. By the last chorus it has crawled out of the dark: you were a spark the whole time.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  8. Wasteland · 10 Years

    The song that broke 10 Years, from The Autumn Effect (2005), and a slow climber: it took 27 weeks to reach No. 1 on the Modern Rock chart. Jesse Hasek's cousin, the actor Brad Renfro, and his long fight with drugs inspired it.

    Official video

  9. Fine Again · Seether

    The lead single from Disclaimer (2002): slow, minor-key, and about holding on to some hope in the middle of a bad stretch. The video had people holding up signs about the worst things in their lives, and those signs became the album's covers.

    Official video

  10. Monster · Skillet

    Skillet's breakthrough, from Awake (2009), and a No. 4 on the Mainstream Rock chart. John Cooper has said the idea came from The Incredible Hulk, and compared it to a 1950s horror film about the beast inside you coming alive.

    Official video

  11. One Good Hour · ZN RockZN Rock

    A pain that turns up at the same time every night, like a spike behind the eye, and a man ready to trade the house for one clean hour without it. It ends smaller and steadier: spend one hour right, then another.

  12. Nightmare · Avenged Sevenfold

    The title track and lead single of Nightmare (2010), the first single the band put out after drummer The Rev died. It's sung from inside hell, by the ones already there, telling a new arrival he has nobody to blame but himself.

    Official video

  13. Them Bones · Alice in Chains

    The opening song on Dirt (1992), with its verses in 7/8. Jerry Cantrell wrote it thinking about mortality: everybody ends up a pile of bones one day, so enjoy the time you've got.

    Official video

  14. You Don't Get My Day · ZN RockZN Rock

    A pain that moved in behind the eye like a lodger and never takes a day off. The song lets it stay and refuses it the rest: it can have the pain, not the way you live your day.

  15. Black Hole Sun · Soundgarden

    The third single from Superunknown (1994). Chris Cornell called it a surreal dreamscape, mostly playing with words, and said he had no idea how you'd take it literally. Everything in it sounds like the end of the world, and none of it explains itself.

Questions

What are some rock songs with dark lyrics?

From the 2000s: My Immortal by Evanescence, Animal I Have Become by Three Days Grace, Inside the Fire by Disturbed and Nightmare by Avenged Sevenfold. From the 90s: Them Bones by Alice in Chains and Black Hole Sun by Soundgarden. Newer ones include I'm Just Static and Still A Spark by ZN Rock.

Which 2000s rock songs have the darkest meanings?

Inside the Fire by Disturbed comes from a real suicide in David Draiman's teenage years, Animal I Have Become was written in rehab, and Wasteland by 10 Years was inspired by Brad Renfro's fight with drugs. My Immortal and Nightmare are dark by story rather than by biography.

Are these good songs for October or Halloween?

Nightmare, Them Bones, My Immortal and Monster suit a dark October night. Most of the others are dark because of what the people who wrote them went through, so they work any time of year.

If one of these songs is hitting too close: in the US you can call or text 988, any time, day or night.
