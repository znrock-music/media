Rock Songs About Depression and Getting Through It

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

Depression in a rock song hardly ever goes by its name. It shows up as a ceiling you stare at, a smile you put on before work, a stretch of bad weeks nobody can explain. Most of these were written by people who went through it, and four are mine, tagged ZN Rock. You get the story behind each song and a link to it.

  1. Fell on Black Days · Soundgarden

    The last single from Superunknown (1994). Chris Cornell said it came from noticing his outlook could turn dark with no breakup, no accident, nothing to point at, and how frightening that was. He wanted a song about exactly that.

    Official video

  2. Unwell · Matchbox Twenty

    From More Than You Think You Are (2002). Rob Thomas has described it as being at odds with yourself and feeling like a freak, and he calls it a positive song in the end, because you work out you're not crazy.

    Official video

  3. Life Is Beautiful Anyway · ZN RockZN Rock

    The blinds stay shut, the ceiling is the same gray every morning, and you're carrying something you can't name. The chorus keeps saying life is beautiful while admitting it can't see it, until the last one, where the light gets in through the cracks.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. Today · The Smashing Pumpkins

    From Siamese Dream (1993), and the first song Billy Corgan wrote for it. He has said he was in a suicidal place at the time and found it funny to call a day the greatest of your life because it couldn't get any worse. The bright tune is the joke.

    Official video

  5. Away from the Sun · 3 Doors Down

    The title track of the band's 2002 album, and a single in 2004. Brad Arnold said it came from two or three years when he kept ending up back at the bottom, as if every climb out got him pushed back in.

    Official video

  6. I'm Just Static · ZN RockZN Rock

    Nine a.m., a voice for the room and another one for yourself, and a smile you hang up like a coat. Everyone sees the picture and nobody sees the crack, until the song lets the whole act break, loud.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  7. Nutshell · Alice in Chains

    From Jar of Flies (1994), the first EP ever to debut at No. 1 on the Billboard 200. Layne Staley sings about fighting a battle alone with no one to turn to, and the band opened their 1996 MTV Unplugged show with it.

    Official video

  8. Help! · The Beatles

    The title song of the 1965 film and album. In 1980 John Lennon said he really was crying out for help, calling it his fat Elvis period, and wished they'd recorded it slower, closer to how he felt.

    Official video

  9. Still A Spark · ZN RockZN Rock

    Three in the morning in a comment thread, a landlord in your skull charging rent you can't cover, and buying quiet with the thing that makes it louder. The last chorus stops asking why and finds you were a spark the whole time.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  10. Hurt · Nine Inch Nails

    The closing song on The Downward Spiral (1994), which Trent Reznor made while struggling with depression and drugs. Quiet, close and bleak, it's someone counting up the damage. Johnny Cash's cover gave it a second life in 2002.

    Official video

  11. Everybody Hurts · R.E.M.

    From Automatic for the People (1992). The words are about as direct as rock lyrics get, spoken straight to someone who thinks they're the only one feeling this way: you're not, so hang on.

    Official video

  12. Still Standing Right Here · ZN RockZN Rock

    Written off before the bell, and one of the voices saying it was your own. Then you wake up breathing. The song has been to the bottom, knows the sound of it, and isn't going back down.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  13. Monsters · Shinedown

    A No. 1 on Billboard's Mainstream Rock chart from Attention Attention (2018), an album that follows one man fighting his demons inside a single room. Brent Smith was newly sober and bassist Eric Bass was dealing with depression while they made it.

    Official video

  14. You're Only Human (Second Wind) · Billy Joel

    A 1985 single and a No. 9 hit in the US. Joel wrote it for young people struggling with depression, said he'd come close to suicide himself as a teenager, and gave his earnings from it to the National Committee for Youth Suicide Prevention.

    Official video

  15. I Am a Rock · Simon & Garfunkel

    The closing track on Sounds of Silence (1966) and a No. 3 hit in the US. A man walls himself off so nothing can reach him, and decides feeling nothing is safer than getting hurt again.

    Official video

Questions

What are some good rock songs about depression?

Fell on Black Days by Soundgarden, Today by The Smashing Pumpkins, Unwell by Matchbox Twenty, Nutshell by Alice in Chains and Everybody Hurts by R.E.M. are some of the best known. Life Is Beautiful Anyway by ZN Rock is a newer one about holding on until the light gets in.

Are there rock songs about overcoming depression?

Unwell by Matchbox Twenty and You're Only Human (Second Wind) by Billy Joel both end on hope. Still Standing Right Here and Life Is Beautiful Anyway by ZN Rock are about getting back up after hitting the bottom.

What's a rock song about pretending to be okay?

I'm Just Static by ZN Rock is about the smile you put on at nine a.m. and the real you shouting underneath it. Today by The Smashing Pumpkins hides a dark day under a bright tune.
