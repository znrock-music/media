Rock Songs About Holding a Grudge and Forgiveness

A song list · 14 songs · updated October 2026

Holding a grudge feels like holding power, right up until you notice it only weighs on you. Rock has plenty of songs from both ends of that: the bitter ones, the apologies, and the ones that finally let go. Two of the songs here are mine, tagged ZN Rock, about nine years of anger at an old friend and about someone who stayed anyway. The rest are by bands you know, with a note on each and a link to play it.

  1. Don't Look Back in Anger · Oasis

    From (What's the Story) Morning Glory? (1995), and a UK No. 1 in early 1996. It was the first Oasis single sung by Noel Gallagher instead of Liam, and it's about shrugging off what's behind you.

    Official video

  2. Nine Years In · ZN RockZN Rock

    Nine years of staying angry at an old friend over something small, while he never even felt it. The song is about who a grudge really costs, and calling someone while you still can.

    Lyric videoLyricsAdd it to your Liked SongsApple MusicYouTube Music

  3. Go Your Own Way · Fleetwood Mac

    The first single from Rumours (1977). Lindsey Buckingham wrote it about the end of his relationship with Stevie Nicks, and the band kept recording and touring together anyway.

    Official video

  4. Sweet Home Alabama · Lynyrd Skynyrd

    From Second Helping (1974). It's usually told as a feud with Neil Young, because the song mentions him by name. In fact the two were on good terms, and Young has called it a great song.

    Official video

  5. One · U2

    From Achtung Baby (1991). The band argued over the direction of the record in Berlin and nearly split, and this song came out of that stretch. It's about people who have hurt each other and still have to carry on together.

    Official video

  6. Here Today · Paul McCartney

    From Tug of War (1982). McCartney wrote it after John Lennon was killed, as the conversation he never got to finish, including the part where you say you loved them.

    Official video

  7. Tell Me Why You Stay · ZN RockZN Rock

    A thank-you to someone who stayed through the silent weeks and the worst of you. It says out loud that their staying is what saved you.

    Lyric videoLyricsAdd it to your Liked SongsApple MusicYouTube Music

  8. Hard to Say I'm Sorry · Chicago

    Written by Peter Cetera and David Foster for Chicago 16 (1982), and a US No. 1. An apology that won't come out easily, and a plea not to leave before it does.

    Official video

  9. It's Been Awhile · Staind

    Staind's biggest hit, from Break the Cycle (2001). Aaron Lewis looks at the people he let down and the damage he did to himself, with no excuses attached.

    Official video

  10. What I've Done · Linkin Park

    The lead single from Minutes to Midnight (2007), also heard in the first Transformers film. It's about facing what you've done and wanting a clean slate.

    Official video

  11. Perfect · Simple Plan

    From No Pads, No Helmets...Just Balls (2002). A son tells his father he's sorry he couldn't be the kid he wanted, and wishes they could start over.

    Official video

  12. Good Riddance (Time of Your Life) · Green Day

    Billie Joe Armstrong wrote it about an ex-girlfriend who moved to Ecuador, and he has said he was angry about it. It came out on Nimrod (1997), and it's a soft-sounding goodbye that is partly a rebuke.

    Official video

  13. How to Save a Life · The Fray

    A No. 3 hit on the Hot 100 from the band's 2006 debut. Isaac Slade has said it grew out of mentoring a troubled teenager, and trying to find the right thing to say.

    Official video

  14. Cat's in the Cradle · Harry Chapin

    Chapin's only US No. 1 (1974). A father is always too busy for his son until the son grows up just as busy, and by then the call is hard to make.

    Official video

Questions

What are some rock songs about holding a grudge?

Nine Years In by ZN Rock is about nine years of anger at a friend over something small. Sweet Home Alabama by Lynyrd Skynyrd and Go Your Own Way by Fleetwood Mac are two famous songs that came out of friction between people who knew each other.

What are rock songs about forgiveness?

Don't Look Back in Anger by Oasis, One by U2 and What I've Done by Linkin Park are the best known. Here Today by Paul McCartney is about the conversation he never had with John Lennon.

What are rock songs about apologizing?

Hard to Say I'm Sorry by Chicago, It's Been Awhile by Staind and Perfect by Simple Plan are all apologies in different tones. Tell Me Why You Stay by ZN Rock is the thank-you for someone who stayed.
