2000s Rock Songs About Making It and Going Home

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

Leaving home to chase something, getting part of the way, and thinking about the people you left. A lot of the big 2000s rock ballads are about exactly that, and so are a few of my songs, which are marked ZN Rock. Every song has a line on what it's about and a link to play it.

  1. Home · Daughtry

    Chris Daughtry came to fame on American Idol, and this 2007 single became the song the show played when contestants were sent home. It's about being worn out from chasing something and heading back to the people who know you.

Questions

What are some 2000s rock songs about going home?

Home by Daughtry, When I'm Gone and Here Without You by 3 Doors Down, and Photograph by Nickelback are the classics. Tell Him I Made It and Somewhere Safe Again by ZN Rock are newer.

What rock songs are about chasing your dreams?

Second Chance by Shinedown is about leaving home to go after a life in music, and Rockstar by Nickelback pokes fun at the dream. My Long Way Around by ZN Rock is about the wrong turns on the way.
