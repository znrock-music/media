Rock Songs About Anxiety and Overthinking

A song list · 14 songs · updated October 2026

Rock songs for a head that won't switch off. Most of this list is the 2000s stuff you already know by heart. A few are my own songs as ZN Rock, and they're marked so you can tell. Each one has a line on what it's about and a link, and both playlists sit at the bottom if you'd rather press play.

  1. Numb · Linkin Park

    The closing song on Meteora (2003). It's about wearing yourself out trying to be what someone else expects of you, until you can't feel anything about it at all.

    Official videoSpotify

  2. Stressed Out · Twenty One Pilots

    From Blurryface (2015), the album named after a character Tyler Joseph uses to stand for his insecurities. It's about missing being a kid now that money and other people's opinions keep you up.

    Official videoSpotify

  3. Still A Spark · ZN RockZN Rock

    Sleepless nights, a head that starts turning on you, and trying to buy quiet with the thing that makes it worse. The one idea it holds on to: you're still a spark, and you can walk yourself home.

    Lyric videoLyricsSpotifyApple Music

  4. Breath · Breaking Benjamin

    A single from Phobia (2006) that spent seven weeks at No. 1 on Billboard's Mainstream Rock chart. Something, or someone, is slowly taking the air out of your life, and the song never says which.

    Official videoSpotify

  5. Breaking the Habit · Linkin Park

    A Meteora single from 2004, written by Mike Shinoda and sung by Chester Bennington. It's about a self-destructive loop you can't explain and can't seem to leave.

    Official videoSpotify

  6. I'm Just Static · ZN RockZN Rock

    Wearing a smile and a clean version of yourself for everyone while the real you is shouting underneath. It ends with tearing the mask down and picking a mess that's your own.

    Lyric videoLyricsSpotifyApple Music

  7. Scars · Papa Roach

    From Getting Away with Murder (2004), and a No. 15 hit on the Billboard Hot 100. It's about caring too much and the marks that leaves, the kind that don't fade when you want them to.

    Official videoSpotify

  8. Outside · Staind

    Aaron Lewis first played it alone on the 1999 Family Values Tour, finishing the words as he sang, with Fred Durst on backing vocals. The studio version on Break the Cycle (2001) is about being on the outside, looking in.

    Spotify

  9. Heavy · Linkin Park feat. Kiiara

    The lead single from One More Light (2017). Chester Bennington sings about problems stacking up inside his own head, most of them the kind that get heavier the more you think about them.

    Spotify

  10. Late Night Roll Call · ZN RockZN Rock

    For the nights your head won't shut off and you end up in a loud online room full of regulars. They argue about everything, settle nothing, and never miss a night.

    Lyric videoLyricsSpotifyApple Music

  11. Crawling · Linkin Park

    From Hybrid Theory (2000), and the song that won Linkin Park the Grammy for Best Hard Rock Performance. It's about losing control to something inside you and not trusting your own head.

    Spotify

  12. The Red · Chevelle

    Chevelle's breakout single from Wonder What's Next (2002). Pete Loeffler wrote it about getting so angry you literally see red, and having to step back from whatever keeps feeding it.

    Spotify

  13. Still Standing Right Here · ZN RockZN Rock

    Everyone wrote you off, you wrote yourself off too, and you're still here. A song about everything that should have broken you and didn't.

    Lyric videoLyricsSpotifyApple Music

  14. Never Too Late · Three Days Grace

    From One-X (2006). Adam Gontier has said it started out being about suicide and how it's never too late to change where you are, and that it later became more about just being present.

    Spotify

Questions

What are some good rock songs about anxiety?

Numb and Crawling by Linkin Park, Stressed Out by Twenty One Pilots, Breath by Breaking Benjamin and Never Too Late by Three Days Grace are some of the best known. Newer ones include Still A Spark and I'm Just Static by ZN Rock.

Which Linkin Park songs are about anxiety and overthinking?

Crawling, Numb, Breaking the Habit and Heavy all deal with a mind that turns on itself. Heavy, from 2017, is the most direct about overthinking.

Is there a playlist of these songs?

Yes. The list is a YouTube playlist (official videos) and a longer 20-song Spotify playlist, both embedded on this page.
