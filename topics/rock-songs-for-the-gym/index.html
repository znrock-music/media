Rock Songs for the Gym and the Last Rep

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

The last rep is where a song earns its place on a gym playlist. It needs a pulse, a chorus you can shout at the weights and something that makes quitting feel like a bad option. This list goes from Survivor and AC/DC to Skillet and Imagine Dragons. Two songs are mine, tagged ZN Rock, loud shout-alongs. They weren't written as workout songs, but they work for that. Each song has a note and a link to play it.

  1. Eye of the Tiger · Survivor

    Written for Rocky III (1982) after Sylvester Stallone asked the band for a song. It spent six weeks at No. 1 in the US and still sounds like climbing back into the ring.

    Official video

  2. Thunderstruck · AC/DC

    The opening track on The Razors Edge (1990), written by Angus and Malcolm Young. It starts with a single guitar line that keeps climbing, which is exactly what a warm-up is for.

    Official video

  3. And We're Never Going Down · ZN RockZN Rock

    A loud roll call of the regulars in a late-night online room who never log off. Nobody agrees on anything, and nobody up at 2 a.m. is on their own. The chorus is made to be shouted.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave this one on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. Seven Nation Army · The White Stripes

    From Elephant (2003), written by Jack White, and the Grammy winner for Best Rock Song. The riff has become a chant sung by crowds at sports events around the world.

    Official video

  5. Welcome to the Jungle · Guns N' Roses

    The opening track on Appetite for Destruction (1987). A warning to anyone new in town, delivered at a speed that has made it a pre-game favorite.

    Official video

  6. Monster · Skillet

    Skillet's breakthrough, from Awake (2009), and a No. 4 on the Mainstream Rock chart. John Cooper has said the idea came from The Incredible Hulk, and the beast inside you coming alive.

    Official video

  7. Back in Black · AC/DC

    The title track of the album the band made months after singer Bon Scott died in 1980, with Brian Johnson stepping in. The all-black cover was a sign of mourning, and the song is a send-off loud enough for him.

    Official video

  8. Believer · Imagine Dragons

    From Evolve (2017). Dan Reynolds sings about taking pain and turning it into fuel, over drums that hit like a workout playlist.

    Official video

  9. Get Up · Shinedown

    From Attention Attention (2018). Brent Smith wrote it while helping bassist Eric Bass through depression, and it's about getting up on the days you don't want to.

    Official video

  10. Don't Stop Me Now · Queen

    From Jazz (1978), written by Freddie Mercury. A full-speed song about feeling unstoppable and wanting nobody to get in the way.

    Official video

  11. Late Night Roll Call · ZN RockZN Rock

    A fast, funny roll call of the regulars in a late-night online room who argue about everything and settle nothing. They never get it right, but they never miss a night.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave this one on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  12. Enter Sandman · Metallica

    The lead single from the Black Album (1991). The riff is one of the most recognisable in metal, and the pace is steady enough to count reps to.

    Official video

  13. Animal I Have Become · Three Days Grace

    The lead single from One-X (2006), the album Adam Gontier wrote after going through rehab. A heavy, angry song with room to push through.

    Official video

  14. Hit Me with Your Best Shot · Pat Benatar

    From Crimes of Passion (1980), written by Eddie Schwartz and a top-10 US hit. The narrator tells whoever's swinging to bring their best, because she isn't going anywhere.

    Official video

  15. Livin' on a Prayer · Bon Jovi

    From Slippery When Wet (1986), written with Richie Sambora and Desmond Child. Tommy's out of work, Gina's pulling shifts at the diner, and they hang on to each other. The key change is made for the final set.

    Official video

Questions

What are the best rock songs for the gym?

Eye of the Tiger by Survivor, Thunderstruck by AC/DC, Seven Nation Army by The White Stripes and Welcome to the Jungle by Guns N' Roses are the usual picks. And We're Never Going Down by ZN Rock is a newer shout-along.

What rock songs are good for the last rep?

Anything with a chorus you can shout. Don't Stop Me Now by Queen, Believer by Imagine Dragons and Livin' on a Prayer by Bon Jovi all build toward a big finish.

What rock songs have a fast tempo for running?

Late Night Roll Call by ZN Rock, Enter Sandman by Metallica and Back in Black by AC/DC all keep a driving pace.
