Rock Songs About Friendship

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

Love songs get most of the attention in rock, but there's a whole shelf about friends: the old gang back in town, the one who shows up when it's bad, the one you stopped calling. This list runs from Queen and Thin Lizzy to the Rolling Stones and Billy Joel. Four of the songs are mine, tagged ZN Rock, about grudges, old friends and the regulars of a late-night room. Every song comes with a note and a link to play it.

  1. With a Little Help from My Friends · The Beatles

    Ringo Starr sings it as Billy Shears, the second song on Sgt. Pepper (1967), coming straight out of the title track's applause. Joe Cocker's slowed-down cover a year later became an anthem of the Woodstock era.

    Official video

  2. Friends Will Be Friends · Queen

    A 1986 single from A Kind of Magic, written by Freddie Mercury and John Deacon. On the Magic Tour it was sung at the end of the shows, squeezed in between We Will Rock You and We Are the Champions.

    Official video

  3. Nine Years In · ZN RockZN Rock

    One small falling-out with an old friend became nine years of silence, and the friend barely noticed. The song works out who the grudge was costing and dials the number.

    Lyric videoLyricsAdd it to your Liked SongsApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. The Boys Are Back in Town · Thin Lizzy

    The first single from Jailbreak (1976), sung by Phil Lynott, about the old crowd getting back together. It's played at most Irish rugby matches, and Rolling Stone ranks it the band's best song.

    Official video

  5. You've Got a Friend · James Taylor

    Written by Carole King for Tapestry (1971). Taylor's version, with Joni Mitchell on backing vocals, went to No. 1 in the US, and the song won King the Grammy for Song of the Year. It's a promise to show up whenever you're needed.

    Official video

  6. Waiting on a Friend · The Rolling Stones

    From Tattoo You (1981), although it was started in Jamaica in 1972, with Sonny Rollins on saxophone. Jagger said the lyric was about friendships in the band, and in the video he waits in a doorway for Keith Richards.

    Official video

  7. The Thing I'd Never Say · ZN RockZN Rock

    A toast to the regulars of a late-night online room who joke, argue and never ask how you're doing. It's the thank-you nobody in that room would ever say into a mic.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  8. Bridge over Troubled Water · Simon & Garfunkel

    Written by Paul Simon and sung by Art Garfunkel, the title track of their last studio album (1970). It held No. 1 in the US for six weeks, won five Grammys, and it's about being there when someone is at their lowest.

    Official video

  9. Scenes from an Italian Restaurant · Billy Joel

    A seven-minute piece from The Stranger (1977). Two old classmates meet over dinner, and the middle section tells the story of Brenda and Eddie, the high school couple everyone remembers.

    Official video

  10. My Friends · Red Hot Chili Peppers

    A ballad from One Hot Minute (1995), and the only song from that album on the band's Greatest Hits. It was their first single to top both of Billboard's rock charts, and it's about sticking by friends when they're down.

    Official video

  11. I Keep Saying I'll Be Happy When · ZN RockZN Rock

    Happiness kept getting pushed to next year, right up to an old friend's funeral. Hearing what people remember about him changes the plan, and the song stops putting life off.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  12. Friend of a Friend · Foo Fighters

    Dave Grohl wrote it in 1990 about his first impressions of his new Nirvana bandmates, Kurt Cobain and Krist Novoselic, on a guitar he found in Cobain's apartment. It finally landed on a Foo Fighters album with In Your Honor (2005).

    Official video

  13. He Ain't Heavy, He's My Brother · The Hollies

    Written by Bobby Scott and Bob Russell, who met only three times while Russell was dying of lymphoma. The Hollies' 1969 version was a worldwide hit and reached No. 1 in the UK when it was reissued in 1988. It's about helping someone along without counting the cost.

    Official video

  14. You're My Best Friend · Queen

    From A Night at the Opera (1975), written by John Deacon for his wife, and a No. 7 hit in the UK. Proof that the best friend in a song can be the person you married.

    Official video

  15. And We're Never Going Down · ZN RockZN Rock

    A loud roll call of a late-night online room where nobody logs off and nobody agrees on anything. What holds the crowd together is that no one awake at 2 a.m. is left on their own.

Questions

What are some good rock songs about friendship?

With a Little Help from My Friends by The Beatles, Friends Will Be Friends by Queen, The Boys Are Back in Town by Thin Lizzy and Waiting on a Friend by The Rolling Stones are the best known. Nine Years In by ZN Rock is about a friendship lost to a grudge.

What are rock songs about a friendship ending?

Nine Years In by ZN Rock is about a friendship that ended over something small, and Scenes from an Italian Restaurant by Billy Joel looks back at school friends who went separate ways. There's also a separate list of rock songs about friends drifting apart.

What are rock songs about friendship and memories?

The Boys Are Back in Town by Thin Lizzy and Scenes from an Italian Restaurant by Billy Joel are both about old friends getting together again. I Keep Saying I'll Be Happy When by ZN Rock is about what an old friend's funeral makes you remember.
