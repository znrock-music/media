Rock Songs About Road Trips and Driving at Night

A song list · 13 songs · updated October 2026

Some songs only fully work with a dark road and a windshield. This list is for the long drive: the open highway in the daytime, the empty one after midnight, and the radio you can't turn down. It runs from Springsteen and the Eagles to AC/DC and Bob Seger. One song is mine, tagged ZN Rock, about the wrong turns on the way here. Every song has a note on what it's about and a link to play it.

  1. Born to Run · Bruce Springsteen

    The title track of the 1975 album that put Springsteen on the map. A couple in a small town talk about getting into a car and not coming back.

    Official video

  2. Take It Easy · Eagles

    The band's first single (1972), written by Jackson Browne and Glenn Frey. It's the song with the corner in Winslow, Arizona, and it's about easing off and letting the road do the worrying.

    Official video

  3. Highway to Hell · AC/DC

    The title track of the 1979 album, the last the band made with Bon Scott, and one of the most played road songs in rock.

    Official video

  4. Hotel California · Eagles

    The title track of the 1976 album, written by Don Felder, Don Henley and Glenn Frey, and the Grammy winner for Record of the Year. It opens with a drive down a dark desert highway, one of the best night-drive scenes in rock.

    Official video

  5. My Long Way Around · ZN RockZN Rock

    Every wrong road and dead-end town, and not being sorry for any of it. A song for the drive where you stop wishing you'd taken a shorter way.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  6. Turn the Page · Bob Seger

    A song about life on tour, with the long empty highways, the bad hotels and the stares. It first appeared in 1973, but the live version from Live Bullet (1976) is the one most people know.

    Official video

  7. Running on Empty · Jackson Browne

    The title track of a 1977 album recorded entirely on tour: on stage, backstage, in hotel rooms and on the bus. It's about a life that keeps rolling faster than you can take stock of it.

    Official video

  8. Life Is a Highway · Tom Cochrane

    From Mad Mad World (1991), a No. 1 in Canada. Rascal Flatts covered it for the film Cars in 2006, so there are two road-trip singalong versions for two generations.

    Official video

  9. Runnin' Down a Dream · Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers

    The riff-driven single from Full Moon Fever (1989), written by Petty, Jeff Lynne and Mike Campbell. A song about chasing something just out of reach.

    Official video

  10. Night Moves · Bob Seger

    The title track of Seger's 1976 album, looking back on a teenage summer. In the last verse he's woken by thunder in the middle of the night and lies there remembering.

    Official video

  11. Here Without You · 3 Doors Down

    From Away from the Sun (2002). A road ballad about being far from someone you love and still feeling them with you. It suits the stretch of the drive after midnight.

    Official video

  12. Car Radio · Twenty One Pilots

    First released in 2011 and re-recorded for Vessel (2013). Tyler Joseph's car radio really was stolen, and the song is about the silence that leaves you alone with your thoughts.

    Official video

  13. Don't Stop Believin' · Journey

    From Escape (1981), a No. 9 hit about two strangers taking a midnight train and a chance. It's on this list because every car sings along to it.

    Official video

Questions

What are the best rock songs for a road trip?

Born to Run by Bruce Springsteen, Take It Easy by the Eagles, Highway to Hell by AC/DC and Life Is a Highway by Tom Cochrane are the classics. My Long Way Around by ZN Rock is a newer one about the wrong turns on the way.

What are good rock songs for driving at night?

Hotel California by the Eagles, Turn the Page by Bob Seger and Here Without You by 3 Doors Down all suit an empty road after dark. Night Moves by Bob Seger is for the memory-lane drive.

What rock songs are about the road and touring?

Turn the Page by Bob Seger and Running on Empty by Jackson Browne were both written about life on tour.
