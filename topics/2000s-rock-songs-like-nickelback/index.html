2000s Rock Songs Like Nickelback

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

If How You Remind Me and Photograph are on your playlist, this is the neighborhood: big choruses, plain words, radio rock from the 2000s that sounds best at full volume. It starts with two Nickelback songs and moves to bands that sat next to them on the radio. Three songs here are mine, tagged ZN Rock, in the same lane of big hooks and plain language. Each has a note and a link to play it.

  1. How You Remind Me · Nickelback

    The US No. 1 from Silver Side Up (2001) that broke the band worldwide, and the most-played song on US radio in the 2000s. Chad Kroeger wrote it after a fight with his then-girlfriend, and it takes as much of the blame as it hands out.

  2. Here Without You · 3 Doors Down

    From Away from the Sun (2002). A road ballad about being far from someone you love and still feeling them with you.

    Official video

    Everyone wrote you off, you wrote yourself off too, and you're still here. A song about everything that should have broken you and didn't.

  4. Fine Again · Seether

    The lead single from Disclaimer (2002): slow, minor-key, and about holding on to some hope in the middle of a bad stretch. The video had people holding up signs about the worst things in their lives.

    Official video

  5. It's Been Awhile · Staind

    Staind's biggest hit, from Break the Cycle (2001). Aaron Lewis looks back at addiction, a relationship he wrecked, and how much of the damage he did to himself.

    Official video

  6. So Cold · Breaking Benjamin

    The lead single from We Are Not Alone (2004), and the record holder for most weeks on Billboard's Mainstream Rock chart, 62. Benjamin Burnley wrote it during a New York winter, cut off from everyone.

    Official video

  7. Home · Daughtry

    Chris Daughtry came to fame on American Idol, and this 2007 single became the song the show played when contestants were sent home. It's about being worn out from chasing something and heading back to the people who know you.

    Official video

  8. Tell Him I Made It · ZN RockZN Rock

    Looking at an old photo of the kid you used to be and wishing you could tell him it worked out. A message back through the years: hold on, the worst of it ends.

  9. Photograph · Nickelback

    From All the Right Reasons (2005). Chad Kroeger flips through old pictures and remembers growing up in Hanna, Alberta, the small town he left behind.

    Official video

  10. When I'm Gone · 3 Doors Down

    From Away from the Sun (2002), with a video shot aboard the aircraft carrier USS George Washington. It's about asking someone to hold on to you while you're away.

    Official video

  11. Unwell · Matchbox Twenty

    From More Than You Think You Are (2002). Rob Thomas has described it as being at odds with yourself and feeling like a freak, and he calls it a positive song in the end, because you work out you're not crazy.

    Official video

  12. Wherever You Will Go · The Calling

    From Camino Palmero (2001), and 23 weeks at No. 1 on Billboard's Adult Top 40. Co-writer Aaron Kamin has said it came from seeing a widower at a funeral.

    Official video

  13. Scars · Papa Roach

    From Getting Away with Murder (2004), and a No. 15 hit on the Billboard Hot 100. It's about caring too much and the marks that leaves.

Questions

What bands are like Nickelback?

3 Doors Down, Seether, Staind, Breaking Benjamin, Daughtry, Matchbox Twenty and Papa Roach all sat on the same radio playlists in the 2000s. ZN Rock is a newer act writing rock in a similar lane of big choruses and plain words.

What are good 2000s rock songs like How You Remind Me?

Fine Again by Seether, It's Been Awhile by Staind and Unwell by Matchbox Twenty are the closest in mood: a slow verse, a big chorus and a narrator being honest about his own part.

What are rock songs like Photograph by Nickelback?

Home by Daughtry looks back at heading home to the people who know you, and Tell Him I Made It by ZN Rock looks at an old photo of the kid you used to be.
