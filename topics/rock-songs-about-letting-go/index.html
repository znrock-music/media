Rock Songs About Letting Go

A song list · 13 songs · updated October 2026

Letting go doesn't happen in one go. It's a grudge, a bad year, a person, or just the plan you had for how things were supposed to turn out. These are rock songs about all of it, from calm acceptance to a loud goodbye. Three are mine, tagged ZN Rock, and none of them claim it's easy. Every song has a note on it and a link to play it.

  1. Let It Be · The Beatles

    Released in 1970, written by Paul McCartney. He has said it came from a dream about his mother, who died when he was a teenager, telling him it would be all right.

    Official video

  2. Don't Look Back in Anger · Oasis

    From (What's the Story) Morning Glory? (1995), written by Noel Gallagher, who sings lead on it. It reached No. 1 in the UK in 1996 and is sung back by whole crowds.

    Official video

  3. Nine Years In · ZN RockZN Rock

    Nine years of staying angry at an old friend over something small. It asks who the grudge was really hurting, and then picks up the phone.

    Lyric videoLyricsAdd it to your Liked SongsApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. You Can't Always Get What You Want · The Rolling Stones

    The closing track of Let It Bleed (1969), written by Mick Jagger and Keith Richards. It opens with a choir and settles on the idea that needs and wants are different things.

    Official video

  5. Take It Easy · Eagles

    The band's first single (1972), written by Jackson Browne and Glenn Frey. It's a road song about easing off, and not letting the worry run the day.

    Official video

  6. Free Bird · Lynyrd Skynyrd

    From their 1973 debut album, written by Allen Collins and Ronnie Van Zant. A goodbye to someone who can't be held, ending in one of the longest guitar solos in rock.

    Official video

  7. Truce for Tonight · ZN RockZN Rock

    After years of swearing it's the last time and being wrong by midnight, this one quits the fight to win. It lets go of the big promise and keeps a small one: today.

    Lyric videoLyricsAdd it to your Liked SongsApple MusicYouTube Music

  8. Wildflowers · Tom Petty

    The title track of Petty's 1994 solo album, produced by Rick Rubin. It's a gentle goodbye that tells someone to go and find the place where they feel free.

    Official video

  9. Walk · Foo Fighters

    From Wasting Light (2011), and the winner of the Grammy for Best Rock Performance in 2012. It's about losing your way and finding the road back, one step at a time.

    Official video

  10. Dust in the Wind · Kansas

    From Point of Know Return (1977), written by Kerry Livgren. A quiet acoustic song about how little of anything lasts, and a No. 6 hit in the US.

    Official video

  11. Somewhere Safe Again · ZN RockZN Rock

    It starts at 3 a.m. with strangers shouting and the bottle running the night. The song is about finding a spark, leaving that room and walking back to somewhere safe.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave this one on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  12. Black · Pearl Jam

    From Ten (1991), with lyrics by Eddie Vedder. A song about watching someone you love go, and hoping that they have a good life without you.

    Official video

  13. Wish You Were Here · Pink Floyd

    The title track of the 1975 album, written by David Gilmour and Roger Waters. It's about the people who aren't in the room anymore.

    Official video

Questions

What are some good rock songs about letting go?

Let It Be by The Beatles, Don't Look Back in Anger by Oasis, Free Bird by Lynyrd Skynyrd and Wildflowers by Tom Petty are the best known. Nine Years In by ZN Rock is about dropping a grudge before it takes any more years.

What are rock songs about letting go of the past?

Don't Look Back in Anger by Oasis and Walk by Foo Fighters are about moving on from what happened. Somewhere Safe Again by ZN Rock is about walking away from the worst nights.

What's a rock song about accepting things you can't change?

You Can't Always Get What You Want by The Rolling Stones and Let It Be by The Beatles are the classic ones. Truce for Tonight by ZN Rock settles for one day at a time.
