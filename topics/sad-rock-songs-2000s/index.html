Sad Rock Songs of the 2000s

A song list · 16 songs · updated October 2026

Plenty of sad rock songs from the 2000s aren't about breakups at all. They're about a father who died, a grandmother you couldn't get home to, an apology that came late. Here are the ones that hold up, with a few older ones that never stopped being played. Four are mine, tagged ZN Rock, and they're the quiet kind of sad. Every song comes with its story and a link.

  1. Wake Me Up When September Ends · Green Day

    From American Idiot (2004), and a single in 2005. Billie Joe Armstrong wrote it about his father, who died of cancer in September 1982 when Billie Joe was ten. The video, with Jamie Bell and Evan Rachel Wood, follows a couple split up by the Iraq War.

    Official video

  2. My Immortal · Evanescence

    Amy Lee and Ben Moody wrote it when they were fifteen, and the version on Fallen became the album's third single in December 2003. A loss that won't let go, with a piano that does most of the work.

    Official video

  3. Nine Years In · ZN RockZN Rock

    The sad part isn't the fight, it's the nine years after it. A friend of twenty years, one remark at a birthday, and a phone nobody picked up. He moved on and the grudge didn't, so the last line is just: call while they'll still answer.

  4. It's Been Awhile · Staind

    The lead single from Break the Cycle (2001) and Staind's biggest hit, No. 5 on the Hot 100. It spent 20 weeks at No. 1 on Billboard's Mainstream Rock chart, and Aaron Lewis sings it as a run of apologies to people he let down.

    Official video

  5. Hurt · Johnny Cash

    Cash recorded Trent Reznor's song for American IV (2002), and the 2003 video by Mark Romanek, shot partly in his own museum with June Carter Cash beside him, became its own piece of art. She died that May and he followed in September.

    Official video

  6. How to Save a Life · The Fray

    The second single from the band's debut (2006), and a No. 3 hit on the Hot 100 that stayed on the chart for 58 weeks. Isaac Slade has said it came from mentoring a troubled teenager, and trying to find the right thing to say.

    Official video

  7. Tell Me Why You Stay · ZN RockZN Rock

    A thank-you that came late. Someone kept the kitchen light on through weeks of silence and the worst version of you, and the song finally says out loud that their staying is what saved you.

    Lyric videoLyricsAdd it to your Liked SongsApple MusicYouTube Music

  8. Helena · My Chemical Romance

    From Three Cheers for Sweet Revenge (2004), and a single in 2005. Gerard and Mikey Way wrote it for their grandmother, who died while the band was on tour and couldn't visit her in her last year. The video was filmed in a Los Angeles church.

    Official video

  9. Broken · Seether feat. Amy Lee

    Written years earlier for Seether's Disclaimer (2002), then redone with Amy Lee for The Punisher soundtrack in 2004, and a No. 20 hit in the US. Shaun Morgan has said it's about the pain of leaving someone behind, written for his newborn daughter.

    Official video

  10. Blackbird · Alter Bridge

    The title track of the band's 2007 album, a ballad of about eight minutes. Myles Kennedy wrote the words for a friend, Mark Morris, who ran a guitar shop in Spokane, Washington and sold him his first guitar, after he died.

    Official video

  11. Life Is Beautiful Anyway · ZN RockZN Rock

    Gray mornings, a weight with no name and a bottle that calls like a friend, and a chorus that insists life is beautiful anyway. It stays sad for most of the song, and then the light starts getting in.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave this one on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  12. Adam's Song · blink-182

    From Enema of the State (1999), and the third single in 2000. Mark Hoppus was the main writer, and it was the most serious thing the band had put out up to then: suicide, depression and loneliness, with a piano in the bridge.

    Official video

  13. Boulevard of Broken Dreams · Green Day

    From American Idiot (2004), and the Grammy winner for Record of the Year in 2006. A man walks an empty road by himself and the only shadow beside him is his own.

    Official video

  14. Black · Pearl Jam

    The fifth track on Ten (1991), with words by Eddie Vedder and music by Stone Gossard. It's about a relationship ending and the way a memory loses its color, and fans have kept it a favorite for decades.

    Official video

  15. I Keep Saying I'll Be Happy When · ZN RockZN Rock

    A funeral for a man you came up with, where nobody mentions the work, only his Sundays and the way he stayed. The song walks out of it and stops putting happiness on next year's list.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  16. Fix You · Coldplay

    From X&Y (2005). Chris Martin wrote it for Gwyneth Paltrow after her father died, on a keyboard her father had bought not long before. It starts out slow and sad and only lifts at the end.

    Official video

Questions

What are the saddest rock songs of the 2000s?

Hurt by Johnny Cash, Wake Me Up When September Ends by Green Day, My Immortal by Evanescence and How to Save a Life by The Fray are the ones most often named. Nine Years In by ZN Rock is a newer one, about a friendship lost to a grudge.

What's a sad rock song about losing someone?

Wake Me Up When September Ends is about Billie Joe Armstrong's father, Helena by My Chemical Romance is for the Way brothers' grandmother, and Blackbird by Alter Bridge is for a friend who sold Myles Kennedy his first guitar.

Are there sad rock songs that end on hope?

Fix You by Coldplay lifts at the end, and Boulevard of Broken Dreams never does. Life Is Beautiful Anyway by ZN Rock stays in the dark most of the way and lets the light in at the end.
