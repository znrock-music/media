Rock Songs About Addiction and Recovery

A song list · 12 songs · updated October 2026

Rock has always had songs about using and about getting clean, and the good ones never preach. This is a list of those, plus four of mine marked ZN Rock. Each entry says what the song is about and where to play it. Both playlists are further down.

  1. Under the Bridge · Red Hot Chili Peppers

    From Blood Sugar Sex Magik (1991). Anthony Kiedis wrote it about the loneliness of getting clean, and about the place in Los Angeles where he had used and nearly lost everything.

    Official videoSpotify

  2. It's Been Awhile · Staind

    Staind's biggest hit, from Break the Cycle (2001). Aaron Lewis looks back at addiction, a relationship he wrecked, and how much of the damage he did to himself.

    Official videoSpotify

  3. Truce for Tonight · ZN RockZN Rock

    Years of swearing today is the day and losing it by midnight. The song gives up on winning the whole war and makes a smaller deal: one day standing, just today.

    Lyric videoLyricsAdd it to your Liked SongsApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. Animal I Have Become · Three Days Grace

    The lead single from One-X (2006), the album Adam Gontier wrote after going through rehab. It's about not recognising who you turn into when you're using.

    Official videoSpotify

  5. Not a Word I Own · ZN RockZN Rock

    A headache that keeps its own schedule, and a faster way to shut it out that never knows when to stop. No promises in it, just being up and awake today.

    Lyric videoLyricsIf it sticks, save it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  6. Breaking the Habit · Linkin Park

    From Meteora (2003). It's about wanting out of a habit that's taking you apart and not understanding why you keep going back.

    Spotify

  7. Still No End In Sight · ZN RockZN Rock

    A weekend that turns into two days awake and a mind that turns a room against you. It ends with one day down and saying out loud that you can't do it alone.

    Lyric videoLyricsIf it sticks, save it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  8. Gravity · Papa Roach feat. Maria Brink

    From F.E.A.R. (2015). Jacoby Shaddix wrote it about the lowest point of his marriage, when he'd let down the people closest to him, and his wife being what pulled him back.

    Spotify

  9. So Far Away · Staind

    From 14 Shades of Grey (2003). Aaron Lewis looks back at a darker stretch of his life from somewhere steadier, and at how far away all of it feels now.

    Spotify

  10. Somewhere Safe Again · ZN RockZN Rock

    Three in the morning, a room full of arguing strangers, and the powder and the bottle taking over. It's about digging for a spark in the dark and walking yourself back somewhere safe.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave this one on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  11. Pain · Three Days Grace

    Also from One-X. It's about choosing to feel something, even if it hurts, over the numbness.

    Spotify

  12. Never Too Late · Three Days Grace

    One more from One-X. Gontier wrote it about how it's never too late to change the situation you're in.

    Spotify

Questions

What rock songs are about addiction?

Under the Bridge by Red Hot Chili Peppers, It's Been Awhile by Staind and Animal I Have Become by Three Days Grace are the best known. Not a Word I Own and Still No End In Sight by ZN Rock are newer ones.

What rock songs are about recovery and staying sober?

So Far Away by Staind and Gravity by Papa Roach look back from the other side. Truce for Tonight by ZN Rock is about taking it one day at a time.

Is there a playlist?

Yes, a YouTube playlist of the official videos and an 18-song Spotify playlist, both embedded on this page.
