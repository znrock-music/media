Rock Songs About Gratitude and Saying Thank You

A song list · 12 songs · updated October 2026

Thank you is a hard thing to sing without going soft. The good ones manage it: a toast, a letter to a wife, a sarcastic goodbye that's really a thank-you anyway. Three songs here are mine, tagged ZN Rock, for the people who stayed when there was no reason to and the crowd that carried me without knowing it. The rest are by bands you know, with a note on each and a link to play it.

  1. Thank You · Led Zeppelin

    From Led Zeppelin II (1969). Robert Plant wrote the words for his wife Maureen, and it was the first lyric he wrote on his own for the band, over John Paul Jones's organ.

  2. The Thing I'd Never Say · ZN RockZN Rock

    A toast to a late-night online room that the narrator would never make with a microphone in his hand. "You don't know the half of what you've pulled me through" and, at the end, "Here's to every one of you."

  3. Thank U · Alanis Morissette

    The lead single from Supposed Former Infatuation Junkie (1998), written after a trip to India. A list of thank-yous to the things that hurt, including disillusionment and terror, and the video had her walking city streets with nothing on.

  4. You're My Best Friend · Queen

    From A Night at the Opera (1975). John Deacon wrote it for his wife Veronica and played the electric piano on it; it was a top-ten single in the UK and a top-20 hit in the US.

  5. Tell Me Why You Stay · ZN RockZN Rock

    To the person who sat across the table through the closed-door weeks. "That the only thing that saved me / Was the day you chose to stay." The last verse admits it should have been said years ago.

  6. Thank You for Being a Friend · Andrew Gold

    A No. 25 hit in 1978 from All This and Heaven Too. Seven years later a version sung by Cynthia Fee became the theme of The Golden Girls, which is how most people know it now.

  7. Kind and Generous · Natalie Merchant

    From Ophelia (1998), her second solo album after 10,000 Maniacs. A plain thank-you song with a wordless chorus you can sing after one listen.

  8. My Best Bad Idea · ZN RockZN Rock

    A toast to the wrong turns. "Here's to every wrong turn, every burned bridge, every scar" and to the woman who took one look at a mess and decided she could work with it.

  9. Thank You for Loving Me · Bon Jovi

    From Crush (2000), the follow-up single to It's My Life. A ballad written by Jon Bon Jovi and Richie Sambora for whoever stuck around through the bad years.

  10. Thank You Friends · Big Star

    The closing track of Third, recorded in Memphis in 1974 and not released until 1978. Alex Chilton's thank-you comes out somewhere between sincere and bitter, and the song has since been covered as a straight tribute.

  11. I Thank You · ZZ Top

    The opening track of Degüello (1979) and a top-40 single the next year. A cover of the 1968 Sam & Dave soul hit written by Isaac Hayes and David Porter, which Billy Gibbons heard on the car radio while the band was recording in Memphis.

  12. Thnks fr th Mmrs · Fall Out Boy

    From Infinity on High (2007), with the vowels stripped out of the title. A thank-you to an ex that is at least half an insult, and one of the band's biggest hits.

Questions

What are some rock songs about gratitude?

Thank You by Led Zeppelin, Thank U by Alanis Morissette, You're My Best Friend by Queen, Kind and Generous by Natalie Merchant and Thank You for Loving Me by Bon Jovi are the ones that come up most. Thank You for Being a Friend by Andrew Gold is the Golden Girls one.

What are rock songs that say thank you to a partner who stayed?

Tell Me Why You Stay by ZN Rock is a thank-you to someone who stayed through a hundred reasons to leave. Thank You by Led Zeppelin and You're My Best Friend by Queen were both written for the singers' wives.

What are rock songs that thank your friends?

The Thing I'd Never Say by ZN Rock is a toast to a room of online friends, and My Best Bad Idea by ZN Rock is a toast to the wrong turns that got him here. There is also a longer list of rock songs about friendship, and one of songs about online friends.
