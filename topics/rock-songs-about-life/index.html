Rock Songs About Life

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

Rock songs about life tend to go one of two ways. Some tell you to grab it while you can, and some stop halfway and wonder how you ended up here. This list has both, from Bon Jovi and Kansas to Talking Heads and Queen. Four of the songs are mine, tagged ZN Rock, about the crooked version of it. Each has a short note on what it's about and a link to play it.

  1. It's My Life · Bon Jovi

    A single from Crush (2000), written by Jon Bon Jovi, Richie Sambora and Max Martin. It nods to Frank Sinatra's My Way and to Tommy and Gina from Livin' on a Prayer, and it reached No. 3 in the UK.

    Official video

  2. Once in a Lifetime · Talking Heads

    From Remain in Light (1980). David Byrne delivers it like a sermon, and it's about looking up one day at the house, the family and the job and asking how you got there.

    Official video

  3. My Long Way Around · ZN RockZN Rock

    Shortcuts it could have taken and towns it could have quit in, and it kept driving anyway. Its one lesson: nothing gets learned when everything goes right, it all gets learned in the hard stretches.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  4. Don't Stop Believin' · Journey

    From Escape (1981), written by Steve Perry, Neal Schon and Jonathan Cain. Cain took the title from what his father kept telling him while he was struggling as a musician in Los Angeles.

    Official video

  5. Bitter Sweet Symphony · The Verve

    From Urban Hymns (1997), built on strings sampled from an orchestral version of the Rolling Stones' The Last Time. It opens on a life spent working for money until it ends, and the sample cost Richard Ashcroft the writing credit until 2019, when it was handed back.

    Official video

  6. Against the Wind · Bob Seger

    The title track of the 1980 album, with Glenn Frey of the Eagles on backing vocals, and a No. 5 hit in the US. Seger took running against the wind from his high school cross-country days and made it a picture of getting older. It's also the song Forrest Gump runs to.

    Official video

  7. Nobody Gets to Keep It · ZN RockZN Rock

    Here happiness isn't somewhere you arrive. It turns up in an ordinary hour, over a coffee going cold, and it's gone by evening, and the song decides that it coming back round is enough.

    Lyric videoLyricsAdd it to your Liked SongsApple MusicYouTube Music

  8. Carry On Wayward Son · Kansas

    From Leftoverture (1976), written by Kerry Livgren and the last song brought to the sessions. Livgren called it a song of self-encouragement about keeping on looking, and it became the band's first top 40 hit.

    Official video

  9. If Today Was Your Last Day · Nickelback

    From Dark Horse (2008), and a No. 19 hit on the Hot 100. It asks what you'd do differently if today were all you had left, and Chad Kroeger has called its middle part very motivational.

    Official video

  10. Learning to Fly · Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers

    From Into the Great Wide Open (1991), written by Petty with Jeff Lynne. It spent six weeks at No. 1 on US rock radio, and it's about getting on with life when the coming down is the hard part.

    Official video

  11. My Best Bad Idea · ZN RockZN Rock

    A man who was a mess, a woman who decided she could work with that, and a loud house with nothing on the walls. Every good thing in the song came from getting it wrong first.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  12. Times Like These · Foo Fighters

    From One by One (2002). Dave Grohl wrote it during a break when the band wasn't sure it had a future, about learning to live again. In 2020 a BBC Radio 1 all-star cover went to No. 1 in the UK for COVID-19 charities.

    Official video

  13. Meant to Live · Switchfoot

    The lead single from The Beautiful Letdown (2003), written by Jon and Tim Foreman, and No. 18 on the Hot 100. Jon Foreman has said it's about hoping your life is for more than arguments and failed tries.

    Official video

  14. The Show Must Go On · Queen

    Released as a single in October 1991, about six weeks before Freddie Mercury died. Brian May wrote most of it and Mercury sang it while seriously ill: keep the act going, even breaking inside.

    Official video

  15. Life Is Beautiful Anyway · ZN RockZN Rock

    The title says it, and most of the song can't see it: closed blinds, the same gray ceiling, a bottle that wants to be your friend. It keeps saying it anyway, and at the end the light finds a way in.

Questions

What are some good rock songs about life?

It's My Life by Bon Jovi, Don't Stop Believin' by Journey, Once in a Lifetime by Talking Heads and Bitter Sweet Symphony by The Verve are the best known. Life Is Beautiful Anyway by ZN Rock is about holding on to the idea on days you can't see it.

What are rock songs about life being hard?

Against the Wind by Bob Seger, The Show Must Go On by Queen and Times Like These by Foo Fighters all came out of hard stretches. My Long Way Around by ZN Rock is about not trading the hard road for an easier one.

What are rock songs about life lessons?

Carry On Wayward Son by Kansas, Learning to Fly by Tom Petty and If Today Was Your Last Day by Nickelback each carry one. My Best Bad Idea by ZN Rock is about mistakes that turned out right, and Nobody Gets to Keep It is about happiness coming and going.
