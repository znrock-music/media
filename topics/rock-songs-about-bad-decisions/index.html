Rock Songs About Bad Decisions That Worked Out

A song list · 14 songs · updated October 2026

Some of the best things in life came from a call that everybody said was a mistake. Rock has always had a soft spot for the wrong turn: the detour, the burned bridge, the second chance. This list mixes songs that regret it, songs that shrug it off and songs that are glad it happened. Two are mine, tagged ZN Rock, about a bad idea that turned out right and a long way around that nobody would trade. Each song has a note and a link to play it.

  1. You Can't Always Get What You Want · The Rolling Stones

    The closing track of Let It Bleed (1969), written by Mick Jagger and Keith Richards, with a choir at the start. Not getting what you wanted turns out to be different from not getting what you needed.

    Official video

  2. My Best Bad Idea · ZN RockZN Rock

    A toast to the worst advice ever taken and every wrong turn, burned bridge and bad call along the way. Everyone said it would go wrong, and it turned out right.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  3. Carry On Wayward Son · Kansas

    Kerry Livgren brought it in late for Leftoverture (1976), and it became the band's first Top 40 hit. A wanderer who made every mistake is told to keep going, because there will be peace in the end.

    Official video

  4. Once in a Lifetime · Talking Heads

    From Remain in Light (1980). David Byrne delivers it like a sermon about waking up one day in a house, a job and a life and asking how you got there.

    Official video

  5. My Back Pages · Bob Dylan

    From Another Side of Bob Dylan (1964), later a hit for The Byrds. Dylan turns on his own younger self, a kid who was certain about everything, and decides he's younger now than he was then.

    Official video

  6. The Middle · Jimmy Eat World

    From Bleed American (2001), and a No. 5 hit on the Hot 100. The band had been dropped by their label and paid for the album themselves, and the song is the pep talk: it'll be fine, just give it time.

    Official video

  7. My Long Way Around · ZN RockZN Rock

    Every wrong road and dead-end town, and not being sorry for any of it. It's about keeping every busted part of the hard way you came.

    Lyric videoLyricsSave it on SpotifyApple MusicYouTube Music

  8. Against the Wind · Bob Seger

    The title track of Seger's 1980 album, his only No. 1 record. He looks back on a life of running against everything and admits he learned some things he'd rather not know.

    Official video

  9. Don't Stop · Fleetwood Mac

    Written by Christine McVie for Rumours (1977). It looks forward to tomorrow and leaves yesterday where it is, and it was later used for Bill Clinton's 1992 campaign.

    Official video

  10. Times Like These · Foo Fighters

    From One by One (2002). Dave Grohl wrote it in 2001, when the band came close to splitting up, about learning how to start over.

    Official video

  11. It's My Life · Bon Jovi

    The lead single from Crush (2000), written by Jon Bon Jovi, Richie Sambora and Max Martin. A shout about living your own way and not apologizing for how it turned out.

    Official video

  12. Glory Days · Bruce Springsteen

    The first verse is a real night in 1973, when Springsteen ran into an old Little League teammate in a bar and they talked for hours about school and baseball.

    Official video

  13. Meant to Live · Switchfoot

    The lead single from The Beautiful Letdown (2003), No. 18 on the Hot 100. Jon Foreman has said it's about hoping your life is for more than arguments and failed tries.

    Official video

  14. Good Riddance (Time of Your Life) · Green Day

    The acoustic song from Nimrod (1997) that closes a bad chapter by wishing it well. It has been played at graduations and farewells, although it began as a bitter goodbye.

    Official video

Questions

What are some rock songs about bad decisions?

Once in a Lifetime by Talking Heads and My Back Pages by Bob Dylan look back at where past choices led. My Best Bad Idea by ZN Rock is a toast to the worst advice ever taken, which still turned out right.

What are rock songs about second chances?

The Middle by Jimmy Eat World, Don't Stop by Fleetwood Mac and Times Like These by Foo Fighters are all about starting again. Carry On Wayward Son by Kansas is about a wanderer being told to keep going.

What are rock songs about not regretting your mistakes?

It's My Life by Bon Jovi shrugs them off, and My Long Way Around by ZN Rock is about keeping every wrong turn. For the other side, there's also a list of rock songs about regret.
