Songs for When You Can't Sleep

A song list · 15 songs · updated October 2026

For the hours when the house is asleep and your head isn't. Some of these are about nights without sleep, some are just good company at 3 a.m., and the last few are quiet enough to drift off to. Three are mine, tagged ZN Rock. Each one has a note on what it's about and a link.

  1. 3AM · Matchbox Twenty

    From Yourself or Someone Like You (1996). Rob Thomas wrote it with his bandmates about living with his mother's cancer when he was a teenager, and the small hours that came with it.

    Official video

  2. Enter Sandman · Metallica

    The lead single from the Black Album (1991). It takes the bedtime story about the Sandman and turns it into a child's nightmare, about the least restful way to say goodnight.

    Official video

  3. Late Night Roll Call · ZN RockZN Rock

    It's three in the morning somewhere and nobody in this online room is heading to bed. A quick, joking roll call of the regulars, who fix the world by two and take it apart again tomorrow.

  4. Lullaby · Nickelback

    A piano ballad from Here and Now (2011). It talks to someone in a dark place who can't see a way out and asks them to hold on.

    Official video

  5. Boulevard of Broken Dreams · Green Day

    From American Idiot (2004), and the Grammy winner for Record of the Year. Walking an empty street at night with nobody but your own shadow.

    Official video

  6. Still A Spark · ZN RockZN Rock

    A comment thread full of strangers at three in the morning, a landlord in your skull charging rent you can't pay, and two days going by without sleep. By the last chorus you've walked yourself home.

  7. Night Moves · Bob Seger

    The title track of Seger's 1976 album, looking back on a teenage summer. In the last verse he's woken by thunder in the middle of the night and lies there remembering.

    Official video

  8. Sleep · My Chemical Romance

    From The Black Parade (2006). Gerard Way wrote it about the night terrors he had while the band was making the album.

    Official video

  9. One Good Hour · ZN RockZN Rock

    A pain that turns up on the clock every night, then forty hours awake arguing with a screen. It ends by asking for less than everything: one good hour, then another.

  10. Lullaby · The Cure

    From Disintegration (1989), and the band's highest UK chart single at No. 5. Robert Smith tied it to the unsettling bedtime songs his father used to make up, and it's about a spider-man coming for you at night.

    Official video

  11. Why'd You Only Call Me When You're High? · Arctic Monkeys

    From AM (2013). He's the one dialing in the small hours after a night out, and the title is her answer.

    Official video

  12. Car Radio · Twenty One Pilots

    First released in 2011 and re-recorded for Vessel (2013). Tyler Joseph's car radio really was stolen, and the song is about the silence that leaves you alone with your thoughts.

    Official video

  13. Nights in White Satin · The Moody Blues

    From Days of Future Passed (1967). Justin Hayward wrote it at 19, and the title came from a gift of satin sheets.

    Official video

  14. Riders on the Storm · The Doors

    From L.A. Woman (1971), the last song the four Doors recorded together. Real rain and thunder run all the way through it.

    Official video

  15. Golden Slumbers · The Beatles

    From Abbey Road (1969). Paul McCartney found sheet music for Thomas Dekker's 1603 Cradle Song on the piano at his father's house and made his own lullaby out of it.

Questions

What are good rock songs for when you can't sleep?

3AM by Matchbox Twenty, Boulevard of Broken Dreams by Green Day and Night Moves by Bob Seger suit the hour. For something quieter, try Nights in White Satin by The Moody Blues or Golden Slumbers by The Beatles. Late Night Roll Call by ZN Rock is about the people who are still up.

What songs are about 3 a.m.?

3AM by Matchbox Twenty is the obvious one, and Why'd You Only Call Me When You're High? by Arctic Monkeys is about the small hours too. Still A Spark by ZN Rock is set at three in the morning.

Are there rock songs about insomnia?

Sleep by My Chemical Romance comes from Gerard Way's night terrors, and Car Radio by Twenty One Pilots is about thoughts that get loud when it's quiet. One Good Hour by ZN Rock has forty hours awake in it.

If sleep has been missing for weeks, it's worth bringing up with a doctor.
