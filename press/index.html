Bio

ZN Rock writes rock songs about the stuff people don't say out loud: grudges you can't drop, friends you lost, the kid you used to be. Two albums out: Rock X, Vol. 1 and Vol. 2.

Two albums came out in September 2026: Rock X, Vol. 1 (12 songs, 18 Sep 2026) and Rock X, Vol. 2 (10 songs, 24 Sep 2026). That's 22 songs, on every major music app, each with an official lyric video on YouTube.

The songs are about the things people carry quietly: the friend you stopped calling over a grudge (Nine Years In), a pain that keeps its own schedule (You Don't Get My Day, One Good Hour), a message back to the kid you used to be (Tell Him I Made It), sleepless nights and holding on to one spark (Still A Spark), the late-night regulars who keep you standing (The Thing I'd Never Say), the happiness you keep postponing (I Keep Saying I'll Be Happy When), the wrong turns you wouldn't trade (My Long Way Around) and taking it one day at a time (Truce for Tonight).

How it's made

ZN Rock writes the songs and the lyrics, and the music is produced with AI tools (Suno).
