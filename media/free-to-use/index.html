How to use a song

  1. Short video? Pick it from the sound library. On TikTok or CapCut, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts, search the library for ZN Rock plus the song title. Added that way, nothing gets muted or claimed.
  2. Longer video, stream or devlog? Edit the song in like any other track. Still A Spark, Tell Him I Made It and Nine Years In are free MP3 downloads. For every other song, use the app's sound library or play it from the YouTube link next to it.
  3. Credit it. Put Music: ZN Rock - Song Title plus the link https://znrock-music.github.io/media/ in your caption, description or stream panel. The copy button next to each song gives you the exact line.

How to credit

One line, anywhere people can read it: the video description, the caption, a stream panel or your !music command.

Music: ZN Rock - Still A Spark · https://znrock-music.github.io/media/

Using several songs? One line per song, or Music: ZN Rock · https://znrock-music.github.io/media/ once. Tagging the ZN Rock account on that platform instead of writing the link is fine too. If you say the name out loud on stream, that counts.

What's ok and what isn't

Free to do

  • Use any song in YouTube videos and Shorts, Instagram Reels and Stories, TikToks, Twitch and YouTube streams, and game devlogs.
  • Use them in sports, gym and gaming edits, slowed, sped up or bass-boosted inside the edit.
  • Monetize those videos and streams.
  • Cut it, loop it, fade it, talk over it.
  • Keep this permission for as long as your video or VOD is up. I won't pull it later.

Not ok

  • Uploading the song on its own, as audio only or over a still image or lyrics.
  • Remixing it, sampling it or putting it into a song or release of your own.
  • Selling it, or putting it into anything that gets sold: a music pack, a sound library, a sample pack, a licensing site.
  • Claiming it as yours, including registering it with Content ID or any other rights system.
  • Feeding it to an AI system as training data, or claiming rights over anything trained on it.
  • Using it in a game soundtrack, an ad or a film without asking first (email me, I usually say yes).

Plain English version of the deal: non-exclusive, worldwide, free, and I only take it back if the rules above get broken. I keep the copyright; you keep your video. Honest note: one person writes these songs and the lyrics; the music is produced with AI tools (Suno).

What I promise

Slow it down, speed it up, bass-boost it, drown it in reverb, nightcore it: inside an edit that is all fine. I will not claim, mute or strike a video for a speed, pitch, bass or reverb change on these songs, on any platform. A slowed or sped-up version in your edit is not a remix in my book.

Where you can, pick the sound from the TikTok, Instagram or Shorts library (search ZN Rock plus the title) so nothing gets muted. If you upload your own slowed or sped-up audio with the edit and a claim shows up anyway, send me the link and it is released within 48 hours.

Content ID, claims and whitelisting

These songs are in YouTube's Content ID through my distributor. That is what puts them in the Shorts and Reels sound libraries, and it is also why a long video with the song edited in can get an automatic claim. A claim is not a strike: the video stays up, nothing happens to your channel, it only routes the ad money for that one video to me until I release it.

To get your video whitelisted: email zrock2405@gmail.com with the video link and the song name. I release the claim within 48 hours, per video (the tool my distributor gives me works one video at a time, and only once a claim exists, so a video with no claim yet has nothing to release). YouTube can take a day or two more to update. Streaming on Twitch or Kick is not touched by Content ID at all; only a VOD re-uploaded to YouTube can get a claim.

Picking the song from the TikTok, Instagram or Shorts sound library never claims or mutes anything, so for short videos that is the easy route.

Questions

Do I need to ask first?

No. Pick a song and use it. You don't have to tell me, though I'd love to see what you made.

Can I monetize my video?

Yes. If a long YouTube video gets a claim on one of these songs, email me the link and I'll release it, so the video is yours to monetize again.

Why would a YouTube claim show up at all?

The songs are registered with YouTube's Content ID, which is what puts them in the Shorts sound library. The same system can match a video that has the song edited into it. Nothing on your side is wrong when that happens, it just needs releasing.

Does this cover Twitch and Kick?

Yes, play them on stream, in your BRB screen, in your intro. Content ID does not run on Twitch or Kick. If you later upload the stream to YouTube and it gets claimed, send me the link and I release it.

Can I use them in a sports, gym or gaming edit?

Yes, that is exactly what the hook times are for. Slowed, sped up or bass-boosted inside the edit is all fine.

Is this really free? What's the catch?

Free, with a credit and a link. The catch is that I want people to hear the songs. You can't sell the songs or put them in a pack or library, you can't claim them, and you can't train an AI on them.

What about a game soundtrack, an ad or a film?

That's not covered here. Email me first and tell me what it's for.
