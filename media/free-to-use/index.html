For creators and streamers

Free to use

All 22 ZN Rock songs are free to use in your videos, Shorts, Reels, TikToks, streams and devlogs. No sign-up, no fee. All I ask is a credit.

How to use a song

  1. Short video? Pick it from the sound library. On TikTok or CapCut, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts, search the library for ZN Rock plus the song title. Added that way, nothing gets muted or claimed.
  2. Longer video, stream or devlog? Edit the song in like any other track. Still A Spark, Tell Him I Made It and Nine Years In are free MP3 downloads. For every other song, use the app's sound library or play it from the YouTube Music link next to it.
  3. Credit it. Put Music: Song Title by ZN Rock in your caption or description. A link to https://znrock-music.github.io/media/ is welcome but not needed.

What's ok and what isn't

Free to do

  • Use any song in YouTube videos and Shorts, Instagram Reels and Stories, TikToks, Twitch and YouTube streams, and game devlogs.
  • Monetize those videos and streams.
  • Cut it, loop it, fade it, talk over it.

Not ok

  • Uploading the song on its own, as audio only or over a still image or lyrics.
  • Remixing it, sampling it or putting it into a song or release of your own.
  • Claiming it as yours, including registering it with Content ID or any other rights system.

Got a YouTube claim?

Email zrock2405@gmail.com with the video link. I'll release the claim within 48 hours. YouTube can take a day or two more to update the video. Claims only happen when the song is edited into the video, so picking it from the sound library avoids this.

Questions

Do I need to ask first?

No. Pick a song and use it. You don't have to tell me, though I'd love to see what you made.

Can I monetize my video?

Yes. If a long YouTube video gets a claim on one of these songs, email me the link and I'll release it, so the video is yours to monetize again.

Why would a YouTube claim show up at all?

The songs are registered with YouTube's Content ID, which is what puts them in the Shorts sound library. The same system can match a video that has the song edited into it. Nothing on your side is wrong when that happens, it just needs releasing.

Does this cover Twitch?

Yes, play them on stream. If you later upload the stream to YouTube and it gets claimed, send me the link.

What about a game soundtrack, an ad or a film?

That's not covered here. Email me first and tell me what it's for.
